# Research Dossier: App Store Optimization (App Store and Google Play)

> Research pass completed 2026-10-12 for the Ads Master `app-store-optimization` package, building on the ASO sections of the 2026-10-08 `mobile-app-growth` pass. Scope: store search and metadata, keyword research, store creative and conversion, experiments, custom product pages and custom store listings, ratings, reviews and AI review summaries, App Store tags, Ask Play and other store AI surfaces, featuring and events, localization, metadata policy, tools. Evidence labels follow docs/AUTHORING_SPEC.md section 7 plus [Reported, 2026-10] (see Method). Numbers in brackets refer to the Sources list at the end, which uses the same numbering as `skills/app-store-optimization/references/sources.md`.

## Research method and limits

1. The 2026-10-12 pass ran separate searches for Apple and for Google Play and Android. Every 2025 to 2026 finding came from search result summaries; no page, official or not, was read in full.
2. Labeling rule for this pass: [Reported, 2026-10] when the notes give an official page hit plus an independent source, or two dated independent sources; [Unverified] for any single source, including a single official page seen only as a summary; [Contested] where credible sources disagree, with both sides shown. No 2025 to 2026 finding is labeled [Official] on the strength of a summary.
3. Long standing facts (field limits, PPO rules, Guideline numbers, rating prompt rules, vitals thresholds, the Play Metadata policy) keep the labels they carried in the 2026-10-08 pass. Items moved from that pass that this pass did not re-search keep their earlier labels and are marked "moved" in the skill.
4. Not covered here: paid UA and store fees (`research/mobile-app-growth.md`), AI answers outside the stores and assistant directories (`research/ai-search-optimization.md`).

## Executive summary

1. App Store search now has five things on the results page it did not have in 2024: AI generated tags (US), more Apple Ads slots (from 2026-03-03), keyword linked custom product pages in organic results (from 2025-07-30), new search result assets (WWDC26), and, with iOS 27, Personalized Collections with "App Notes" [Reported, 2026-10] [2, 16, 26, 28, 30, 5, 7, 20]. Apple still documents only the name, subtitle, keyword field and primary category as text inputs, plus downloads and ratings as behavior inputs [Official] [1].
2. AI now writes part of the product page on both stores: Apple's review summaries (iOS 18.4, 2025-03) and Google Play's "Users are saying" (late 2025). Neither can be edited; Apple offers a Report a Concern path [Reported, 2026-10] [9, 18, 19, 53, 54]. The practical lever is the reviews themselves.
3. Tags are generated by LLMs from App Store Connect metadata with AI and human curation; developers can deselect but not add; US only per the help page [Reported, 2026-10] [2, 16, 17]. Whether tags affect ranking is not documented [Unverified].
4. Keyword linked custom product pages can rank organically, but the keyword must come from the existing keyword field, each keyword can link to only one page, and the assignment needs review [Reported, 2026-10]. They improve conversion on terms already indexed; they do not add reach. The cap is 70 per Apple's 2025-10-29 notice as reported by vendors, while some pages still say 35 [Contested] [11, 26, 27].
5. Google Play moved discovery toward Gemini: Ask Play became a store wide overlay with Highlights at I/O 2026, Play Console's Gemini drafts keyword targeted custom store listings and translations, and Play Shorts launched for select US developers [Reported, 2026-10] [34, 48, 49]. Vendor tests say Ask Play reads the description, key features, the app's website and reviews [Reported, 2026-10] [51, 52].
6. Play store listing experiments now run one default graphics experiment or up to five localized ones, with up to two variants, on click metrics (install, open, pre-registration clicks), with adjustable confidence and MDE and a 6 month auto stop [Reported, 2026-10] [37, 55, 56]. The 2026-10-08 dispute over the metrics now leans toward the new metrics, because the help page summary itself lists them (confirm in the live console); click wins still need retention checks.
7. Apple added 11 metadata languages on 2026-03-31 for 50 in total across 175 storefronts [Reported, 2026-10] [11]. Cross-localization (which locales a storefront indexes) rests on a 2022 test that predates the new languages [Unverified] [32].
8. Store listings are the largest cited source when ChatGPT recommends apps: about 47.5% of cited sources in a 2026-05 AppTweak study of 125,000+ US responses, open web about 27%, no citation about 22.6% [Study, 2026-05] [59]. In an AppTweak survey of 1,000 US adults, 58% said they had used AI to find or choose an app and 59% check the store after an AI recommendation [Study, 2026-09] [60]. This makes the iOS description, which Apple search ignores, a real asset.
9. Android quality now gates discovery on two fronts: the long standing crash and ANR thresholds [Official] [41, 42], and an excessive partial wake lock metric with possible exclusion from prominent discovery surfaces from 2026-03-01 [Unverified] [43].
10. Screenshot caption indexing on the App Store remains [Contested]: vendors dated a shift to 2025-06; a test of 64 caption phrases across 8 apps found no strong evidence; Phiture reported Apple denied OCR indexing [23, 24].

## Best practice consensus

1. Treat Apple's name, subtitle and keyword field as one budget; never repeat words; research per locale [Official field rules; Practitioner consensus].
2. On Play, cover clusters in natural sentences in the full description with key text in the top half; never pad keywords [Reported, 2026-10] [36].
3. Keep a term map and a change log; change one set at a time; read at 2 to 4 weeks against control terms [Practitioner consensus].
4. Frame 1 states the outcome with real UI; the first three screenshots carry the search result decision [Practitioner consensus].
5. Test concepts before details, to a planned sample, over full weeks, with a downstream quality check [Practitioner consensus].
6. Build custom product pages and custom store listings per search cluster and ad group, measured against the default page [Official features; Practitioner consensus].
7. Prompt for ratings at success moments through system APIs; reply to every low rating fast; never gate or incentivize [Official rules].
8. Write descriptions as plain fact sheets that people and AI readers can quote, consistent with the website [Practitioner consensus, supported by [59]].
9. Fix vitals before metadata [Official thresholds].
10. Localize by research, not translation, starting with markets that score highest on demand, value, winnability and readiness [Practitioner consensus].

| Screenshot caption OCR indexing | Vendors date indexing to 2025-06 [24] | 64 phrase test across 8 apps found no strong evidence; Phiture reports Apple denied it; Apple lists only name, subtitle and keywords [23, 1] | Write captions for people; treat any ranking effect as a bonus |

## What top operators do differently

| Average | Top 1% |
|---------|--------|
| Picks keywords by volume | Judges relevance first, keeps a term map with sources and dates |
| Changes many fields at once | One change set per locale, change log with previous text, control terms |
| Reads ranks after 3 days | Reads at day 14 and 28; extends when an outside event hits the window |
| Reports a blended conversion rate | Reads conversion per source, including AI referrers |
| Builds dozens of CPPs | Builds pages for indexed terms that convert below default, prunes the rest |
| Asks for reviews randomly | Prompts at success moments, replies within the SLA, tracks themes weekly |
| Ignores AI summaries | Records summary text monthly and traces it to review themes |
| Translates listings | Researches each locale, verifies cross-localization with a canary term |
| Writes descriptions for nobody | Writes the plain fact sheet that people, Ask Play and ChatGPT quote |
| Treats vitals as engineering only | Treats a vitals or wake lock breach as an ASO incident |

## Common expensive mistakes

1. Competitor names in metadata (rejection, takedown).
2. Keyword stuffed Play titles and descriptions (Metadata policy, suspension risk).
3. Changing name, subtitle, keywords and screenshots in one release.
4. Judging a change on day 3, or reverting on day 7.
5. Building keyword pages for terms outside the keyword space.
6. Publishing Gemini drafts or machine translations without native and claims review.
7. Gating or incentivizing ratings; resetting the rating as a cover.
8. Leaving promotional text on an expired offer.
9. Optimizing screenshots while vitals breach thresholds.
10. Planning on a 2022 cross-localization matrix for 2026 languages.
11. Instructions to AI systems hidden in descriptions.

## Verification queue

Each item names the cheapest check that settles it (L1 = live console read, L2 = official page read in full, L3 = two independent dated sources, L4 = controlled test).

1. App Store tags: US only still true; ranking or filter effect (L4 with tag deselection is not worth the risk; watch for Apple documentation, L2); inputs beyond metadata (L2).
2. Apple review summaries: storefront list beyond the US, review threshold, refresh cycle (L2, L1 per storefront); the `customerReviewSummarizations` API resource (L2).
3. Custom product page cap: 70 or 35 (L1).
4. Keyword linked CPPs: keyword source rule and one page per keyword (L1 when assigning).
5. Featuring lead time: 2 vs 3 weeks (L2 on both pages).
6. 2026 header and search result assets: specs, supported OS versions and storefronts, availability date (L1, L2).
7. Personalized Collections: placement, markets, inputs (L2 when Apple documents it).
8. Screenshot caption indexing (L4 with unique caption phrases not present in metadata, read over 28 days).
9. Play experiments: limits and metrics (L1).
10. Play wake lock metric: threshold and enforcement (L1 in Android vitals, L2 blog).
11. Ask Play: markets, languages, inputs (L2, L3); Gemini app discovery rollout (L3).
12. Play "Users are saying": threshold, opt out (L2).
13. Cross-localization for the 11 new languages (L4 canary test per storefront).
14. Apple keyword field byte counting for non Latin scripts (L1 by entering a 100 character Turkish or Japanese string).
15. Natural language App Store search since iOS 18.1 (L3).
16. IAP display names as a search input (L4).
