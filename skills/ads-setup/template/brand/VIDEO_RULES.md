# Video Rules

The brand system every video follows. Ideas change from video to video (variety keeps ads from fatiguing); this system does not (consistency is what makes a brand feel deliberate and premium). `video-studio` reads this before every storyboard; `creative-strategy` keeps it current; changes go to `DECISIONS.md`.

## Composition

- One shot, one idea. The key object sits in the centre of the frame or on a clear focal point; the background never competes with it.
- Every scene gets room to breathe: at least `<n>` frames of hold after a key move.
- Motion is eased, never linear; transitions follow the story, not decoration.
