# Benchmarks and Case Studies (Labeled)

> Knowledge as of 2026-10. OpenAI states that no performance benchmarks exist yet across advertisers, industries or campaign types [Official, 2026-09]. Every number below is either an OpenAI statement about the platform, a vendor panel with a partial method, a partner reported result, or an advertiser self report. Benchmarks vary by vertical, geo, season, format and plan mix. Compare a project against its own history first, its Google Search non-brand performance second, and these figures last.

## 1. How to use this module
1. Never set targets from these numbers; use them to sanity check plans and to spot anomalies.
2. Quote the source, date, sample and caveat every time you cite one.
3. Prefer the most recent figure; the platform changed formats and bidding in May, June, August and October 2026.
4. Vendor panels disagree by up to 30x on the same metric; see section 7.

Pattern across sources: partner and OpenAI cited results are positive and recent (with improved measurement and conversion bidding); independent and self reported results from February to July 2026 were weak, especially on conversion rate. Both can be true: performance depends heavily on vertical, offer, landing page and the measurement setup, and the platform changed significantly mid year. No controlled public study meets a high evidence bar: one research effort screened 15 candidate case studies and found none with a control group [Study, 2026-09].
