# Formats and Platform Specs

> Purpose: choose the format that fits the concept and the placement, and deliver files that meet native specs. Specs change: check the official spec pages listed in the Freshness Protocol before final export. Values below are planning values [Official, verify current] unless marked otherwise.

## 1. Format library

| Format | What it is | Strengths | Weaknesses | Best channels |
|--------|-----------|-----------|-----------|---------------|
| UGC (customer or creator style) | Person talks to phone camera about using the product | Native, trust, cheap to iterate | Can look generic if scripted badly; disclosure needed for paid creators | Meta, TikTok, Shorts |
| Creator led | Known creator in their own style, often via partnership ads or Spark Ads | Borrowed trust and audience signal | Cost, rights, less control | TikTok, Instagram, YouTube |
| Founder story | Founder on camera, origin and mission | Differentiation, authenticity, hard to copy | Founder time, not every founder is good on camera | Meta, LinkedIn, YouTube |
| Demo | Product doing the job, step by step | Proof, reduces anxiety | Boring without a hook | All |
| Testimonial | Real customer outcome | Proof, objection handling | Needs real customers and consent | All |
| Us vs them | Side by side comparison | Clear differentiation for solution aware | Legal risk if naming competitors | Meta, TikTok, YouTube, search copy |
| Listicle | "N reasons" in video or carousel | Easy structure, scannable | Can feel like content farm | Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn |
| Problem solution | Agitate then resolve | Works for problem aware | Formulaic if overused | All |
| Static image | Single image with headline and visual | Cheap, fast, strong for offers and proof | Lower ceiling on cold audiences in some accounts | Meta Feed, LinkedIn, Demand Gen, PMax |
| Carousel | Multiple cards | Storytelling, multiple products, education | More production | Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok (photo mode) |
| Meme | Trending meme format with category twist | Cheap, high hook rate, identity | Short half-life, brand fit risk | Meta, TikTok, X |
| Podcast style | Two people talking, captioned clip | Authority, long hold | Needs real expertise | Meta, YouTube, LinkedIn |
| Street interview | Vox pop questions | Pattern interrupt, social proof | Consent and editing heavy | TikTok, Reels |
| Green screen | Creator over a screenshot (review, Reddit, article) | Native, proof oriented | Rights to screenshots, accuracy | TikTok, Reels |
| AI avatar | Synthetic presenter | Fast localization and versioning | Trust risk, disclosure, uncanny | Explainers, localization, B2B tutorials |
| Catalog or DPA | Product feed driven ads with overlays | Scale for large catalogs | Generic without lifestyle and overlays | Meta, TikTok, PMax (with `commerce-feeds`) |

### Static image types (high performers to rotate)

| Static type | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| Headline + hero product | Big claim, product on clean or contextual background |
| Review card | Verbatim review, stars, name initial, product image |
| Comparison table | Us vs category alternative on 3 to 5 criteria |
| Feature callouts | Product with 3 to 5 labeled arrows |
| Before and after | Only where policy allows; avoid in health, weight, skin claims that imply personal attributes |
| Problem visual | Shows the pain (messy, broken, stressed) with a hook line |
| Notes app or text message | Native looking text screenshot style (must not impersonate real people or platforms deceptively) |
| Social post screenshot style | Tweet or Reddit style quote of a real review (real, with permission) |
| Offer card | Price, discount, deadline, guarantee |
| Infographic or stat | One number with source |
| Founder letter | Short note from the founder |
| Meme static | Known format, category twist |
| UGC still | Frame grab from creator video with text overlay |

## 2. Meta (Facebook, Instagram, Threads, Messenger, Audience Network)

| Placement group | Ratio | Recommended pixels | Notes |
|-----------------|-------|--------------------|-------|
| Feed (FB, IG) | 4:5 (vertical) or 1:1 | 1080 x 1350 | 4:5 takes more screen; 1:1 acceptable |
| Stories, Reels | 9:16 | 1080 x 1920 | Keep text and logos out of the top and bottom UI zones (Meta guidance: roughly top 14% and bottom 35% on Reels) |
| Right column, search | 1:1 or 1.91:1 | 1080 x 1080 | Minor placements |
| Carousel cards | 1:1 or 4:5 | 1080 x 1080 or 1080 x 1350 | 2 to 10 cards |

Copy guidance: primary text shows about 125 characters before truncation; headlines around 27 to 40 characters display fully in most placements [Official, Meta Ads Guide, verify]. Lead with the hook line; put the URL or offer in the first 125 characters if it matters.

**Asset customization by placement:** supply a 9:16 and a 4:5 (or 1:1) version of every key asset instead of letting automatic cropping decide. Use placement asset customization in Ads Manager.

**Flexible format and multiple media:** Meta can choose among multiple images or videos in one ad (flexible ad format). Useful at Starter and Growth tiers for exploration; it hides which asset won unless you check asset level breakdowns. Keep concept tests in separate ads.

**Advantage+ creative enhancements:** see [Andromeda and creative diversity](andromeda-and-creative-diversity.md) section 7 and [AI creative production](ai-creative-production.md).

**Partnership ads:** ads run with the creator's handle and the brand's (see [Creators and UGC](creators-and-ugc.md)).

## 3. TikTok

| Item | Value |
|------|-------|
| Ratio | 9:16 (1080 x 1920 recommended) |
| Length | In-feed supports 5 to 60s widely and longer; TikTok guidance has long pointed to roughly 21 to 34s as a sweet spot for in-feed performance [Official, older TikTok guidance, verify] |
| Sound | Sound on is the norm; use Commercial Music Library or original audio |
| Ad text | Short caption; keep the hook in the video, not the caption |
| Safe zone | Keep text away from the right side buttons and the bottom caption area; use TikTok's safe zone overlay |
| Spark Ads | Boost organic posts from the brand or creator account (authorization code from creator) |
| Formats | In-feed, Spark Ads, carousel (photo mode), TopView (reservation), Smart+ campaigns use multiple creatives automatically |

Native TikTok traits: creator talking to camera, fast cuts, text in TikTok native fonts, trends and sounds, POV and duet or stitch styles, humor. Repurposed Meta ads with 4:5 crops and polished brand intros typically underperform. [Practitioner consensus]

Research: TikTok Creative Center (Top Ads, Creative Insights, Trends, Keyword Insights) shows top ads by region and industry.

## 4. YouTube (in-stream, Shorts, in-feed) and Demand Gen

**ABCD framework (Google):**

| Letter | Meaning | Checks |
|--------|---------|--------|
| A: Attract | Hook attention from the start | Tight framing, fast pacing, motion, audio interest, people in the first 5 seconds |
| B: Brand | Brand early and often, visually and audibly | Logo or product in the first 5 seconds, brand mentioned in voiceover, brand present throughout |
| C: Connect | Make people feel and think | Human faces, emotion, humor, one simple message, demos |
| D: Direct | Ask for action | Clear CTA spoken and on screen, end card, offer |

Google and Kantar reported that ads following the ABCDs were associated with about a 30% lift in short-term sales likelihood and a 17% lift in long-term brand contribution [Study, 2022, Google with Kantar; verify on Think with Google]. Use the ABCD check on every YouTube asset.

| Format | Spec planning values |
|--------|---------------------|
| Skippable in-stream | Any length; skip after 5 seconds; 15 to 60s common for performance; 16:9 primary, provide 9:16 and 1:1 too |
| Bumper | 6 seconds, non-skippable |
| Non-skippable in-stream | 15 seconds (longer on connected TV in some markets) |
| Shorts | 9:16 vertical, sound on, native creator style, hook in first second |
| Demand Gen video | Supply 16:9, 1:1 and 9:16 (and 4:5 where accepted) to serve YouTube, Shorts, Discover, Gmail |
| Demand Gen image | 1.91:1, 1:1, 4:5 (and 9:16 where accepted); logo 1:1; headlines and descriptions as text assets |

## 5. Google Responsive Search Ads (RSA) and Performance Max assets

| Asset | Limit [Official, verify] |
|-------|------------------------|
| RSA headlines | Up to 15, max 30 characters each |
| RSA descriptions | Up to 4, max 90 characters each |
| RSA display paths | 2 fields, 15 characters each |
| PMax headlines | Up to 15, 30 characters |
| PMax long headlines | Up to 5, 90 characters |
| PMax descriptions | Up to 5, 90 characters (one should work at 60 characters) |
| PMax business name | 25 characters |
| PMax images | Landscape 1.91:1, square 1:1, portrait 4:5; up to 20 per asset group |
| PMax logos | Square 1:1 and landscape 4:1 |
| PMax videos | Provide your own (16:9, 1:1, 9:16); otherwise Google may auto-generate video from your assets depending on settings |

RSA writing system: see [Copywriting library](copywriting-library.md) section 8. Pin only when legally required. Use Google Asset Studio and generative image features only after the QA in [AI creative production](ai-creative-production.md). Text customization and final URL expansion in AI Max for Search and PMax generate copy from your site: review generated assets and set brand and text guidelines with `google-ads`.

## 6. LinkedIn

| Format | Planning values [Official, verify] |
|--------|----------------------------------|
| Single image | 1.91:1, 1:1, or vertical 4:5 or 1:1.91; 1200 x 627 or 1080 x 1080 |
| Intro text | Up to 600 characters; first 150 characters show before "see more" |
| Headline | Up to 200 characters; keep under 70 |
| Video | 16:9, 1:1, 9:16 vertical; 3 seconds to 30 minutes; captions strongly advised (mostly muted autoplay) |
| Document ads | PDF carousels for lead gen gated or ungated |
| Thought Leader Ads | Promote posts by employees or other members (with permission) as ads |
| Conversation and message ads | Inbox formats; copy discipline matters |

LinkedIn creative traits: speak to a role and a business outcome, use numbers, show real people from the company, document and carousel education, founder or executive POV via Thought Leader Ads. Hand structure and targeting to `linkedin-ads`.

## 7. ChatGPT and assistant ads

OpenAI announced in January 2026 that it would test ads in ChatGPT for logged in adult users in the US on the Free and Go tiers, shown separately from answers and clearly labeled, not placed near sensitive topics, and without sharing chat contents with advertisers [Official, 2026-01, verify current format and eligibility]. Creative implications:

- The ad appears in the context of a specific question. Write copy that answers the intent directly ("Best CRM for a 5 person agency? [Product] sets up in a day"). No hype superlatives.
- Build intent clusters from search query mining and `ai-search-optimization` prompt data; one ad message per cluster.
- Claims and comparisons will be read next to an AI answer that may contradict them: only verifiable claims.
- Format, character limits, image support and policies: verify with `chatgpt-ads` before writing final copy.

## 8. Microsoft Advertising

RSA specs mirror Google's. Microsoft Audience Network and Copilot placements reuse image assets (1.91:1, 1:1, 4:5 where supported). Hand imports and adjustments to `microsoft-ads`.

## 9. Format choice decision tree

1. What is the awareness level of the concept?
   - Unaware or problem aware: video (UGC, founder, street interview, meme, POV), or a problem visual static.
   - Solution aware: demo, us vs them, mechanism explainer, comparison static or carousel.
   - Product or most aware: testimonial, review card, offer card, catalog with overlays.
2. Which placements carry the budget? If over 50% of Meta spend is in Reels and Stories, 9:16 video must lead.
3. What proof exists? Visual product results favor demo; strong reviews favor testimonial and review cards; a credible founder favors founder story.
4. What can be produced this week at this tier? Choose the cheapest format that carries the concept; upgrade after it wins.

## 10. Export checklist

- Master edit at 9:16, plus 4:5 or 1:1 and 16:9 where the channel uses them.
- Captions burned in (and SRT file for YouTube and LinkedIn).
- Safe zones checked with platform overlay.
- Audio levels normalized; licensed music only.
- File named per naming convention; thumbnail or first frame chosen deliberately.
- Product, price and claims match the landing page.
