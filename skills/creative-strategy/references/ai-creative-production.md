# AI Creative Production

> Purpose: use AI where it adds volume, speed and variation without destroying trust or misrepresenting the product. Tools and defaults change monthly: verify features, terms of use and labeling behavior before every new use. Tool facts below are labeled with the date they were last confirmed; platform-native items were re-verified in the 2026-10-08 pass, third-party tool details were not [see research dossier limitations].

## 1. Where AI helps and where it hurts

| Use | Verdict | Why |
|-----|---------|-----|
| VOC analysis, tagging reviews and transcripts | Strong yes | Faster synthesis; keep quotes verbatim |
| Hook and script ideation from the VOC bank | Yes, then human edit | Volume of options; humans pick and sharpen |
| Copy variations (RSA, primary text, headlines) | Yes, with claim review | Platform generated text can drift into unapproved claims |
| Background and setting variations for product stills | Yes, with fidelity QA | New visual worlds cheaply (diversity), but product must stay exact |
| Image to video animation of statics | Test | Cheap motion for Reels and Stories; check product motion realism |
| B-roll generation (textures, environments, abstract mechanism visuals) | Yes | Fills gaps around real footage |
| Voiceover drafts and localization (dubbing, translation) | Yes, with native review | Fast market expansion; check pronunciation and claims per language |
| Resizing, aspect ratio expansion, captions | Yes | Saves editing time |
| AI avatar presenters for explainers, tutorials, internal or B2B education | Situational | Acceptable for informational content with disclosure; weaker for trust-critical persuasion |
| AI "UGC" actors presenting as customers | No for testimonials | A synthetic person describing an experience they never had is a fake testimonial (FTC rule on reviews and testimonials, 2024) and deceptive |
| Synthetic experts, doctors, or celebrities | No | Deceptive, policy violations, likeness rights |
| Fake reviews, fake screenshots of social posts or news | No | Deceptive; FTC and platform policy |
| AI images of the product showing results it cannot deliver | No | Misrepresentation |

Rule: AI may create the stage, the voice draft and the variations. Real customers, real experts and the real product carry the proof.

## 4. The AI assisted production pipeline

1. Concept and script come from the VOC bank (human approved).
2. Capture real assets first: product photos from every angle, real customer clips, founder footage, real expert interviews.
3. Use AI for: background worlds (2 to 5 per concept), b-roll, versions in lengths and ratios, voiceover drafts, localization, hook text variants.
4. Composite the real product into generated scenes rather than generating the product itself.
5. Run QA (section 5). Reject anything that misrepresents the product or a person.
6. Flag in the ad name (`aiG`, `aiV`, `aiE`) and keep provenance metadata (C2PA) where the tool writes it; do not strip it to avoid labels.
7. Test AI-assisted executions against human-shot executions of the same concept to learn the account's own response.

## 5. QA checklist for AI outputs

| Check | Pass condition |
|-------|----------------|
| Product fidelity | Shape, color, label, size, packaging and text match the real product exactly |
| Claims | No new claims appear in generated text, voice or visuals |
| People | No synthetic person presented as a customer, expert or employee; any real person has written consent |
| Hands, faces, text | No artifacts (extra fingers, warped text, uncanny motion) visible at phone size |
| Brand | Colors, fonts, logo usage match `BRAND.md` |
| Context | Settings are plausible and not misleading (no fake locations or events) |
| Audio | Licensed music; synthetic voice disclosed where required |
| Labels | Expected platform AI label behavior understood; additional disclosure added if required |
| Rights | Tool's commercial use terms allow ad use; inputs were owned or licensed |
| Diversity | Output is a new visual world, not a near-duplicate of live ads |

## 6. Prompt patterns

**Background variation for a real product photo (image tools that accept a reference image):**
```
Place the provided product photo, unchanged, on [surface] in [setting] at [time of day],
[lighting], shallow depth of field, photographed on a phone, natural and slightly imperfect,
no added text, no added logos, product label must remain fully legible and unchanged.
```

**B-roll mechanism visual (video tools):**
```
Macro shot, slow push in, [abstract mechanism e.g. fabric fibers being cleaned at a microscopic level],
clean lab aesthetic, 5 seconds, no text, no people, no brand marks.
```

**Hook ideation (LLM, using the VOC bank):**
```
Using only the verbatim quotes below, write 15 hooks for persona [X] at awareness level [Y].
Use at least 6 hook types from this list: [types]. Each hook under 12 words.
Do not invent facts, numbers or claims. Mark which quote each hook comes from.
Quotes: ...
```

**Script draft:**
```
Write a 30 second problem solution script for concept [card]. Spoken by a real customer
in their own words (talking points, not a script to read). Include on-screen text per beat,
payoff of the hook by second 6, one objection handled, CTA. Only use claims from this list: [approved claims].
```

## 7. Testing AI vs human creative

- Compare within the same concept (same angle and persona), not across concepts.
- Primary metric: CPA or ROAS at the concept level; secondary: hook rate, comments sentiment, AI label presence.
- Watch comments for "AI" mentions and negative sentiment; trust damage shows there before it shows in CPA.
- Record findings in memory only when 2+ concepts agree.

## 8. When to switch platform AI enhancements off (recommendation to channel agent)

- Product color or shape accuracy matters (fashion, cosmetics shades, food, furniture).
- Regulated categories where generated text could create claims (health, finance, legal).
- Luxury and premium brands where generated backgrounds look cheap (fashion brands publicly complained in 2025 to 2026 about auto-generated variations).
- During a clean concept test (enhancements add uncontrolled variation). Run them as a separate test instead.

Recheck settings after duplicating ads: practitioners reported enhancements re-enabling on duplicates in 2026 [Practitioner reports, Marketing Brew 2026-04].
