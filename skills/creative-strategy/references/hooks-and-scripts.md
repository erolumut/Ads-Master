# Hooks and Scripts

> Purpose: write first seconds that stop the scroll and bodies that hold attention and sell, with templates by length and platform. Platform specs live in [Formats and platform specs](formats-and-platform-specs.md).

## 1. The three layer hook

Every video hook has three layers that should agree and land within the first 1 to 3 seconds:

| Layer | What it is | Rules |
|-------|-----------|-------|
| Visual hook | The first frame and first motion | Movement in frame 1, a face or a hand doing something, an unexpected object, high contrast. No logo slate, no black fade in |
| Text hook | On-screen text overlay | 3 to 8 words, large, inside safe zones, readable in 1 second, says the hook even with sound off |
| Verbal hook | The first spoken line | Starts on frame 1 (no breath, no "hey guys"), under 12 words |

TikTok has long advised highlighting the key message or product early; TikTok's Creative Center has stated that most of the highest CTR videos highlight the key message within the first 3 seconds [Official, TikTok Creative Center, date not re-verified]. Google's ABCD framework for YouTube says the same as "Attract" [Official, Google]. Treat "first 3 seconds" as the universal rule; on fast feeds aim for the first 1 second.

## 3. Hook writing procedure

1. Pick the concept's persona, awareness level and strongest verbatim quote.
2. Write 10 hooks using at least 5 different types from the taxonomy.
3. Cut any hook that would also fit a competitor's product (too generic).
4. Cut any hook whose promise the body does not pay off by second 5 to 8 (bait).
5. Pick 3 to 5 for production. For each, specify all three layers (visual, text, verbal).
6. Shoot hooks as separate clips so editors can swap them onto the same body (modular production).

## 4. Text overlays

- Hook text: 3 to 8 words, bold sans serif, high contrast, centered in the safe area.
- Keep text and key visuals out of platform UI zones. Meta advises leaving roughly the top 14% and bottom 35% of a 9:16 Reels frame free of text and logos [Official, Meta Ads Guide, verify current values]. TikTok overlays the right side (buttons) and bottom (caption, CTA). Check the current safe zone template for each placement before export.
- Captions: always burn in captions for Feed placements (high share of sound off viewing). For TikTok, native caption style or burned in captions both work; test.
- One idea per text card; change text every 2 to 4 seconds in sync with speech.
- Avoid dense text blocks: they slow reading and hurt hold.

## 5. Sound

| Platform | Default behavior | Implication |
|----------|------------------|-------------|
| TikTok | Sound on culture | Voice, trending or original audio, sound effects carry the ad; use Commercial Music Library for ads |
| Instagram and Facebook Reels | Mostly sound on | Voice plus captions |
| Facebook and Instagram Feed, Stories | Mixed, many muted | Design to work muted: captions and text hook mandatory |
| YouTube in-stream and Shorts | Sound on by default for most viewing | Voice and audio branding matter (ABCD: say the brand) |
| LinkedIn | Mostly muted in feed autoplay | Captions mandatory, text hook, subtitles file |

Music: use licensed tracks (Meta Sound Collection, TikTok Commercial Music Library, licensed stock). Trending commercial songs on organic posts are not cleared for ads.

## 6. Pacing

- Cut or change something visual every 1.5 to 3 seconds on TikTok and Reels (zoom, angle change, b-roll, text change). [Practitioner consensus]
- Slower pacing is acceptable for founder stories, expert explainers and LinkedIn, if the speaker is compelling and the story escalates.
- Remove every pause, breath and filler word in the edit. Jump cuts are native.
- Deliver the hook's promise by second 5 to 8 to protect hold rate.
- Put a re-hook at 40% to 60% of runtime ("but here's the part nobody tells you") on 30s+ ads.

## 7. CTAs

| CTA type | Example | Use when |
|----------|---------|---------|
| Direct | "Tap Shop Now and pick your scent." | Product and most aware |
| Low friction | "Take the 60 second quiz." | Considered purchase, lead gen |
| Risk reversal | "Try it for 30 nights. If you hate it, send it back." | Price or trust objections |
| Urgency (true only) | "Ends Sunday at midnight." | Promotions |
| Social | "Join 40,000 runners who switched." | Community angles |
| Soft (top funnel) | "Follow for part 2." (organic style) | Only when optimizing for engagement; avoid on conversion campaigns |

Say the CTA, show it as text, and end on the product or offer card. For YouTube, Google's ABCD "Direct" means a clear, spoken and written call to action.

## 8. Script templates

### 15 second (Reels, TikTok, Shorts, Stories)
```
0 to 2s   HOOK (visual + text + verbal)
2 to 6s   PROBLEM or PROMISE payoff (show, don't tell)
6 to 11s  PROOF or DEMO (one strong proof point)
11 to 15s CTA + offer card
```

### 30 second problem solution (UGC)
```
0 to 3s   Hook: problem call out
3 to 8s   Agitate: specific verbatim pain, failed solutions
8 to 15s  Introduce product as the new way (mechanism in one line)
15 to 23s Demo + 1 to 2 proof points (review, number, visual result)
23 to 27s Objection handled (price, ease, safety)
27 to 30s CTA + offer
```

### 30 second testimonial
```
0 to 3s   Strongest outcome line from the customer
3 to 10s  Life before (specific)
10 to 20s What changed, what surprised them, product in use
20 to 26s Who they would recommend it to (identity)
26 to 30s CTA with text card and review count
```

### 45 to 60 second founder story
```
0 to 4s   Hook: the moment that started it ("I was fired for...")
4 to 20s  The problem they lived, the failed solutions
20 to 35s The insight and the build (mechanism)
35 to 50s Proof: customers, numbers, demo
50 to 60s Invitation CTA + guarantee
```

### Us vs them (any length)
```
Hook: "[Us] vs [category default]"
Split screen or side by side test on 3 criteria from VOC
Verdict line, proof
CTA
```
Use category comparisons ("regular detergent") unless legal approves naming a competitor. Comparative claims need substantiation.

### Listicle
```
Hook: "[N] reasons [persona] switch to [product]"
Reason 1 (strongest, covers the biggest pain)
Reason 2 (proof)
Reason 3 (objection killer)
CTA
```

### Demo
```
Hook: product doing the most surprising thing it does, frame 1
Steps 1 to 3 in fast cuts, each with a text label
Result shot
CTA
```

### Podcast or interview clip
```
Hook: the most provocative 1 to 2 lines from the conversation, captioned
Context card (who is speaking, why credible)
Story or argument
CTA tied to the idea
```

### YouTube in-stream (skippable, 30 to 60s, ABCD aligned)
```
0 to 5s   Attract: tight framing, motion, a question or tension; brand visible or audible early
5 to 20s  Connect: story or demo, human emotion, one message
20 to 45s Proof and offer
Last 5s   Direct: spoken + written CTA, brand on screen
```

## 9. Modular production (how to get 20 ads from one shoot)

- Shoot 5 to 10 hooks, 2 to 3 bodies, 2 CTAs per concept.
- Editors assemble hook x body x CTA combinations.
- Version lengths: 9 to 15s, 20 to 30s, 45 to 60s from the same footage.
- Version ratios: 9:16 master, 4:5 and 1:1 crops for Feed, 16:9 for YouTube in-stream and Demand Gen.
- Name every module so analysis can attribute performance to hook vs body (see naming conventions).

## 10. Hook iteration matrix (for a winner)

| Variable | Hold constant | Variations |
|----------|---------------|-----------|
| Hook type | Body, CTA | 5 new hooks across 5 types |
| First frame | Hook script | 3 first frames (face, product, result) |
| Text hook | Visual, verbal | 3 text phrasings |
| Talent | Script | 2 to 3 new creators reading the same script |
| Length | Hook, core proof | 15s, 30s, 60s cuts |

Remember: these are iterations. They help a winner but do not add concept diversity.

## 11. Script QA checklist

- Hook works muted? Text hook present and inside safe zone?
- First word spoken on frame 1?
- Promise paid off by second 8?
- One core message, not three?
- Proof is real and approved?
- Objection addressed?
- CTA spoken and shown?
- Brand or product visible early enough for the platform (YouTube ABCD: early and often)?
- Claims on the approved list? Disclosures included (paid partnership, AI where required)?
- Captions accurate?
