# Creators and UGC

> Purpose: build a creator and UGC supply that feeds concept diversity (new faces, new worlds, new voices), with rights and disclosures handled before anything runs as an ad. Campaign mechanics for partnership ads and Spark Ads are executed by `meta-ads` and `tiktok-ads`.

## 1. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| UGC creator | Makes content for the brand to use in ads; usually does not post it to their own audience |
| Influencer or creator partner | Posts to their own audience; may also license content for ads |
| Organic usage | Brand reposts on its own organic channels |
| Paid usage | Content used in ads from the brand's accounts |
| Whitelisting (Meta: partnership ads; TikTok: Spark Ads) | Ads run under the creator's identity (handle and name), with the creator's permission |
| Usage term | How long the brand may run the content in ads |
| Exclusivity | Creator may not work with competitors for a period |
| Raw footage | Unedited clips that editors can recut into new hooks and versions |

## 2. Sourcing channels

| Channel | Best for | Notes |
|---------|----------|-------|
| Existing customers (review requests, post-purchase emails, community) | Most authentic testimonials | Offer product credit or a fee; written consent and release |
| Employees and founders | Founder story, expert, behind the scenes | LinkedIn Thought Leader Ads, Meta and TikTok organic first |
| UGC marketplaces (for example Billo, Insense, Trend, JoinBrands, Cohley) | Volume of UGC style videos | Quality varies; brief tightly; check rights terms |
| TikTok One (TikTok's creator marketplace platform) | TikTok creators with audience data, Spark Ads workflows | Verify current features in TikTok |
| Meta Creator Marketing Hub (Instagram and Facebook creators) | Creator and organic content discovery (including a "predicted affiliate content" filter), content-level permissions with expiry dates, partnership messaging, one-click draft partnership ads | Launched globally 2026-09-15, merging Creator Marketplace and the Partnership Ads Hub [Official, 2026-09, via MediaPost and Marketing Dive] |
| YouTube BrandConnect | YouTube creators | Integrations and Shorts |
| Direct outreach (search hashtags, competitor tags, niche communities) | Niche experts and real users | Slower, higher fit |
| Agencies and talent managers | Bigger creators, scale programs | Higher cost, contracts heavier |
| Affiliate and TikTok Shop affiliates | Commission based content at scale | Coordinate with `tiktok-ads` and `commerce-feeds` |

## 3. Vetting checklist

- Real user of the category (or willing to genuinely use the product for 1 to 2 weeks before filming).
- On-camera delivery: energy, clarity, natural speech; watch 3 recent videos.
- Audience fit (if posting): demographics, geography, comment quality, no engagement pods or bought followers.
- Brand safety: past content scan for conflicts and controversies.
- Production basics: lighting, audio, framing.
- Diversity value: does this person add a persona, age, setting or style not yet in the roster?
- Reliability: deadlines, revision behavior (references from other brands where possible).

## 4. Rates and deal structures (verify current market rates)

| Deal type | Typical structure | Notes |
|-----------|-------------------|-------|
| UGC video, no posting | Flat fee per video, often with hooks and raw footage included | Practitioner reports put many US UGC videos in the low hundreds of dollars per video, with wide variance by creator and market [Unverified] |
| Usage rights extension | Add-on per period (for example 3, 6, 12 months) or perpetual | Price rises with duration, territories, exclusivity |
| Whitelisting or Spark authorization | Add-on fee per period | Separate from content fee |
| Creator posting to audience | Fee scales with audience and engagement; plus usage add-ons | Request recent post insights (reach, saves, shares), not follower count only |
| Affiliate or commission | Percentage of sales, sometimes plus a small base | Works for TikTok Shop and evergreen products |
| Product seeding | Free product, no obligation | Cheap discovery; no guaranteed content; disclosure still needed if they post |
| Retainer | Monthly fee for N videos | Best for top performers; builds a familiar face and iteration speed |

Always pay for raw footage and multiple hooks: they multiply the value of each shoot.

## 5. Contract and rights checklist (before any paid use)

- Deliverables: number of videos, lengths, ratios, hooks per video, raw footage, revision rounds, deadlines.
- Usage: organic, paid, which platforms, which ad accounts, territories, start and end dates, perpetual or limited.
- Whitelisting or Spark: permission, duration, which accounts, whether comments are moderated and by whom.
- Edits: brand may cut, recombine, add captions, music, text, dub, translate, resize. Specify if AI edits (voice, face, dubbing) are allowed. Default: no synthetic alteration of face or voice without separate written consent.
- Likeness and voice: no cloning, no avatar creation from the creator unless explicitly licensed and paid.
- Disclosure: creator will use platform paid partnership tools and clear disclosure on organic posts.
- Claims: creator may only state genuine experiences and approved claims; no medical, financial or guaranteed results claims.
- Exclusivity: category and duration, if any.
- Payment: amount, schedule, kill fee.
- Expiry handling: brand must stop running paid ads at expiry or renew. Track expiry dates in the creator registry; review monthly.
- Releases: model release for any other person appearing; location release where needed; music licensing.

## 6. Meta partnership ads (creator identity on Meta)

- Partnership ads show both the creator's and the brand's identity with a "Paid partnership" label (formerly branded content ads).
- Setup paths include the creator granting permission to the brand (branded content or partnership ad permissions) or sharing a partnership ad code for a specific post. The brand can then run the creator's post, or use its own media with the creator's identity where permitted [Official, verify current flow in Meta Help Center].
- Creator Marketing Hub (2026-09-15): draft a partnership ad from a creator post in one click with a suggested objective, remove copyrighted music and stickers that block ad use, and message creators in the hub; the Creator Marketplace API now covers Facebook creators and a Messaging API was added; partnership ads can also be created through Meta's ads connector for AI agents (the official Meta Ads MCP server) [Official, 2026-09]. Not included: affiliate commissions, order-level creator attribution or performance payments.
- Instagram live video partnership ads: a creator's active livestream can run as a partnership ad in Stories, Reels and Feed, scheduled GA from 2026-09-29; brief live sessions with a product moment in the first minutes and get ad access granted before the stream [Official, 2026-09; availability varies by account].
- Creative use: partnership ads add a new identity to a concept (new talent and new audience association), which supports diversity. Test the same video with and without the creator identity to measure the identity effect.

## 7. TikTok Spark Ads

- Spark Ads run ads from organic posts (brand or creator) with engagement kept on the original post.
- The creator generates an authorization code for a chosen period (TikTok has offered options such as 7, 30, 60 and 365 days) and shares it with the brand [Official, verify current options].
- Track code expiry in the creator registry; renew before winners expire.
- Spark Ads often outperform dark posts on TikTok because they look and behave like native content [Practitioner consensus].

## 8. LinkedIn Thought Leader Ads

- Promote posts from employees or other LinkedIn members (with their approval) as ads.
- Best creative: founder or expert POV posts, customer stories told by a real person, short native videos.
- Coordinate eligibility and approvals with `linkedin-ads`.

## 9. Disclosure rules for creators (summary; details in compliance module)

- FTC Endorsement Guides (revised June 2023): disclose material connections (payment, free product, family or employment) clearly and conspicuously, in the endorsement itself, hard to miss. Use platform paid partnership tools plus clear language such as "#ad" or "Paid partnership with [brand]" in the caption and on screen or spoken for video. Advertisers are expected to monitor creators.
- Endorsements must reflect honest opinions and real experience; results claims must be substantiated and typical, or clearly qualified.
- FTC rule on consumer reviews and testimonials (in effect since October 2024) prohibits fake testimonials, including AI generated ones, and testimonials misrepresenting the experience of the person giving them.
- Outside the US, check local rules (for example UK CMA and ASA guidance, EU consumer law). Hand legal questions to the human.

## 10. Creator testing system

1. Each new creator gets 1 to 2 proven scripts (from winning concepts) plus 1 new concept. This separates creator effect from concept effect.
2. Name ads with the creator token; roll up performance by creator.
3. Promote creators whose hook rate and CPA beat the account median across 2+ videos to retainer.
4. Retire creators who underperform on proven scripts across 2 videos.
5. Keep a minimum of distinct faces live per main ad set (Growth: 3+, Scale: 6+).

## 11. Program by tier

|------|-------------------|-----|----------------|
| Starter | 0 to 2 plus customers and founder | Customers, founder, 1 UGC creator | Paid usage 3 to 6 months, Meta and TikTok |

| Enterprise | 20+ per market | Roster per market and product line, agencies | Negotiated framework agreements |

## 12. Common creator mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Word for word scripts read badly | Talking points plus hooks; let creators speak naturally |
| No raw footage | Always contract raw footage and separate hook takes |
| Usage rights undefined | Contract checklist before payment |
| Running ads after rights expiry | Registry with expiry dates and monthly review |
| Same creator type across all ads | Recruit for diversity: persona, age, setting, style |
| Creators claiming results they did not have | Brief and review for genuine experience only |
