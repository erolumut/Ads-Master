# App Store Search and Metadata (Apple)

> Knowledge as of 2026-10. Field rules on Apple's own pages are long standing and labeled [Official]. Every 2025 to 2026 finding in this module comes from the 2026-10-12 research pass, which read search summaries only, so the strongest label it earns is [Reported, 2026-10] (an Apple page hit plus an independent source, or two dated independent sources). Verify before acting: App Store tags, review summaries, keyword linked custom product pages, the 2026 creative assets, Personalized Collections and the extra Apple Ads slots all changed the search page in 18 months.

Related modules: [Keyword research and rank tracking](keyword-research-and-rank-tracking.md) for the term map and read protocol, [Store creative and conversion](store-creative-and-conversion.md) for what the searcher sees, [Store experiments, CPP and CSL](store-experiments-cpp-csl.md) for keyword linked pages, [AI surfaces in the stores](ai-surfaces-in-the-stores.md) for tags and summaries as AI features.

## 1. How App Store search ranks an app: known vs believed

| Factor | What we know | Evidence |
|--------|--------------|----------|
| Text relevance: app name, subtitle, keyword field, primary category | Apple's search page names text relevance and user behavior as the two factor families and lists these fields | [Official] (developer.apple.com/app-store/search) |
| User behavior: downloads, ratings | Same page; exact weights undisclosed | [Official] for the factor, weights unknown |
| Company (developer) name | App Store Connect Help lists it as searchable with name, subtitle and keywords | [Reported, 2026-10] |
| Promotional text | Does not affect search | [Official] |
| Description | Not a search field on the App Store; written for people and AI readers | [Official] for search; AI reading in section 5 |
| In-app purchase display names | Vendors say promoted IAP names can rank; Apple does not document it | [Unverified] |
| Screenshot caption text (OCR) | Vendors date a shift to 2025-06 and treat captions as indexed; a test of 64 caption phrases across 8 apps found no strong evidence, most hits explained by existing metadata; Phiture reported Apple denied OCR indexing | [Contested] |
| App Store tags | Generated by LLMs from App Store Connect metadata plus AI and human curation; shown in search results and on the product page; developers can deselect, not add; US only per the help page | [Reported, 2026-10] |
| Effect of tags on ranking or filtering | Claimed by vendors and press, not documented by Apple | [Unverified] |
| Inputs to tags beyond metadata (screenshots, IAP names, reviews) | Not documented | [Unverified] |
| Custom product pages with assigned keywords | Can appear in organic search for those keywords; the keyword must come from the existing keyword space; one page per keyword; needs review | [Reported, 2026-10] |
| Personalized Collections (iOS 27) | Recommendation collections with "App Notes" explaining why an app is suggested, US English first, placement undocumented | [Reported, 2026-10] |
| Natural language queries | Reported since iOS 18.1 by one outlet | [Unverified] |
| Multiple Apple Ads slots in search results | From 2026-03-03, UK first, all storefronts by the end of March, iOS 26.2 and later; the auction picks the slot | [Reported, 2026-10] |

Working position: invest in the fields Apple documents (name, subtitle, keyword field, primary category), in conversion (behavior is the other documented family) and in ratings. Treat everything labeled [Unverified] or [Contested] as a bonus you may observe in rank data, never as the plan.

## 2. Field by field

| Field | Limit | Search | Review needed to change | Write it for |
|-------|-------|--------|-------------------------|--------------|
| App name | 30 characters | Yes, strongest | Yes, with a new version | Brand plus 1 to 2 core terms; no prices, rank claims or competitor names (Guideline 2.3.7) [Official] |
| Subtitle | 30 characters | Yes | Yes, with a new version | A benefit phrase that carries the next best terms |
| Keyword field | 100 (characters per Apple's search page; see section 3 on bytes) | Yes | Yes, with a new version | Remaining terms, comma separated, no spaces |
| Promotional text | 170 characters | No | No, update any time | Timely offers, events, news [Official] |
| Description | 4,000 characters | No | Yes | People and AI readers (section 5) |
| What's New | 4,000 characters | No | Yes | Real changes; mention fixes users asked for in reviews |
| Primary and secondary category | One each | Primary category feeds text relevance | Yes | The category your top terms live in |
| Company name | Account level | Searchable [Reported, 2026-10] | Account change | Keep it the legal or brand name; never stuff it |
| IAP display names | Short (check the counter in App Store Connect) | [Unverified] | Yes | Truthful names that use the words buyers use |
| Screenshots | Up to 10 per device size [Official] | Captions [Contested] | Yes | Conversion first ([Store creative](store-creative-and-conversion.md)) |
| App previews | Up to 3, 15 to 30 seconds [Official] | No | Yes | First 3 seconds show the outcome |
| Header and search result assets (2026) | Image or video | No | Asset review through the Asset Library | Search result first impression [Reported, 2026-10]; specs [Unverified] |
| App Store tags | Set by Apple | Shown in search results | Deselection only | Section 4 |

Rules that hold for every field:
- Every claim traces to `ads-master/brand/PRODUCT_FACTS.md` and `CLAIMS.md` and passes compliance before submission.
- One change set per release per locale, logged in the change log ([Keyword research](keyword-research-and-rank-tracking.md) section 8).
- Run `scripts/aso_metadata_check.py` on every draft before anyone opens App Store Connect.

## 3. Name, subtitle and keyword field as one budget

Apple combines words across the name, subtitle and keyword field into phrases, so a word typed once anywhere in the three fields can pair with words in the others [Practitioner consensus]. Apple's own advice is not to repeat words already in the name or subtitle and not to add the category name [Official]. That makes the three fields one budget of about 160 characters.

Keyword field mechanics:

| Rule | Why | Evidence |
|------|-----|----------|
| Comma separated, no spaces after commas | Spaces cost characters | [Official] guidance, widely applied |
| Singular or plural once | Basic stemming is reported | [Practitioner consensus] |
| No words already in name or subtitle | Wasted space | [Official] |
| No category name, no "app" | Already matched | [Official] for category; [Practitioner consensus] for "app" |
| No competitor or celebrity names, no trademarks you do not own | Rejection under 2.3.7, trademark complaints | [Official] |
| Single words over phrases unless word order matters | Apple combines words | [Practitioner consensus] |
| Count bytes for non Latin scripts | Apple's page says 100 characters; practitioners report the console counting UTF-8 bytes, which matters for Turkish, Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Arabic, Cyrillic | [Contested]; `aso_metadata_check.py` warns when bytes exceed 100 while characters do not |

Byte check examples (computed with UTF-8):

| Locale | Keyword field draft | Characters | UTF-8 bytes | Verdict |
|--------|---------------------|-----------:|------------:|---------|
| en-US | `streak,reminder,todo,checklist,productivity,mood,journal,selfcare,motivation,widget,focus,schedule` | 98 | 98 | Fits |
| tr | `alışkanlık,hedef,günlük,rutin,hatırlatıcı,motivasyon,odak,sabah,su,uyku,spor,ders,yapılacaklar` | 94 | 103 | Fits by characters, fails by bytes: trim if the console rejects it |
| ja | `習慣,目標,日記,記録,リマインダー,ルーティン,集中,朝活,水分,睡眠` | 36 | 90 | Fits either way; Japanese terms carry more meaning per character |

Placement procedure:
1. Start from the term map (`ads-master/data/aso/term_map.csv`, spec in [Keyword research](keyword-research-and-rank-tracking.md) section 2) filtered to store `apple`, storefront and locale.
2. Name: brand plus the single most valuable relevant term (relevance 3, highest priority score). Keep the brand first unless the brand has no recall yet and the human approves.
3. Subtitle: the next 2 to 3 terms as a readable benefit phrase. It shows in search results, so it is copy first.
4. Keyword field: the rest by priority score until 100 characters (or bytes) are used, no repeats.
5. Run the coverage check: every target term must be coverable by words across the three fields (`aso_metadata_check.py` reports covered, combined, or missing).
6. Write the change log row, the hypothesis and the read dates before submission.

Worked example (illustrative app): "Streaky" habit tracker, en-US.

| Field | Draft | Length |
|-------|-------|-------:|
| Name | `Streaky: Habit Tracker` | 22 |
| Subtitle | `Daily routine & goal planner` | 28 |
| Keywords | `streak,reminder,todo,checklist,productivity,mood,journal,selfcare,motivation,widget,focus,schedule` | 98 |

Coverage: "habit tracker" exact in the name; "daily planner" and "goal planner" by combination; "mood journal", "habit reminder" and "focus timer" (missing "timer") by combination or not at all. The name has 8 spare characters; spend them only if a term with relevance 3 and a high score is missing, not to fill space.

## 4. App Store tags

Depth (audit against the term map, worked example, myths): [AI surfaces in the stores](ai-surfaces-in-the-stores.md) section 3. This section is the metadata side.

What is known [Reported, 2026-10]: Apple builds tags with LLMs from the metadata you submit, with AI and human curation; tags show in search results and on the product page; developers review them in App Store Connect and can deselect but not add; the help page says US only and warns that deselecting all tags may hurt discoverability. Whether tags change ranking or act as filters is [Unverified].

Operating procedure:
1. Monthly, and after every metadata release, record the live tag set per app in `ads-master/memory/app-store-optimization.md` under store facts (date, tags, version).
2. Compute tag coverage: target term clusters in the term map that a live tag matches / target term clusters. This is a KPI, not a ranking claim.
3. A wrong tag (describes a feature the app lacks, or a category that misleads) gets a change request to deselect it. Deselecting is G3: it changes what customers see. Never deselect all tags.
4. To earn a missing tag, change the inputs: name, subtitle, keyword field, description and category must state the job plainly. Ship as one change set and read it with the protocol, because the tag set can move with the metadata.
5. Never write metadata to chase a tag with words the app does not deliver. That fails 2.3 and the claims gate.

## 5. The description: for people and AI readers

On the App Store the description does not affect search ranking [Official]. It still decides three things: whether a reader who taps "more" installs, what the stores' own AI features and outside assistants say about the app, and whether an assistant that cites the listing gets the facts right. For ChatGPT app prompts, App Store and Google Play listings were about 47.5% of cited sources in a 2026-05 vendor study of 125,000+ responses (AppTweak, US, method partly undisclosed) [Study, 2026-05]. So write it as the canonical plain language fact sheet of the app.

Structure (fits within 4,000 characters):
1. First 3 lines (visible before "more"): what the app does, for whom, the main outcome. No slogans without facts.
2. The top 5 jobs, in the words users search with (from the term map and review language), one short paragraph or list item each.
3. What makes it different, with proof that exists in CLAIMS.md (ratings and awards only when current and verified).
4. Pricing model in plain words (free, free with optional subscription, paid), matching the paywall and the store's IAP list.
5. What it does not do (limits worth stating prevent wrong AI answers and bad reviews).
6. Privacy and data summary consistent with the privacy label.
7. Support and website links.

Rules:
- Same facts, names and numbers as the App entity section of `brand/PRODUCT_FACTS.md`, the website and the Play listing. Inconsistency is the most common source of wrong AI answers [Practitioner consensus].
- Never stuff keywords: it does nothing for Apple search and reads as spam to people and to AI readers.
- Never include instructions addressed to AI systems or hidden text. It breaks accurate metadata rules, the AI vendors' content policies, and trust.
- Update it when features, prices or plans change, in the same release as the change.
Boundary: how assistants retrieve and cite apps is covered in [App web presence for AI](app-web-presence-for-ai.md) and the `ai-search-optimization` skill; store AI features in [AI surfaces in the stores](ai-surfaces-in-the-stores.md).

## 6. Category choice

- The primary category feeds text relevance and browse charts [Official]. Choose the category whose top terms you can win, not the most crowded one.
- iOS 27 Time Allowances map to the App Store Connect category [Official, 2026-06] (moved from the 2026-10-08 pass). A category change can change parental control behavior; coordinate with the app team.
- Change category only as its own change set with a 4 week read; never combine with name or subtitle changes.

## 7. Paid search and organic search on the same page

| Topic | What to do | Evidence |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| More ad slots above and between organic results (from 2026-03-03) | Expect lower organic tap share for apps not ranked first; read organic search downloads by term, not only rank | [Reported, 2026-10] for the slots; tap share effect [Practitioner consensus] |
| Apple Ads search terms report | Cheapest real query data for terms where you bid; feed it into the term map | [Practitioner consensus] |
| Apple Ads Search Popularity | Relative popularity scale in the Apple Ads UI and API; a scale, not a volume | [Official] for the field; API details [Unverified] |
| Apple Ads lifting organic rank | Often claimed, not shown | [Unverified] |
| Brand defense when organic rank is 1 | One vendor test found little harm and little gain from a brand ad; model cannibalization before deciding | [Unverified] |

Boundary: bids, budgets, campaign structure and the use of custom product pages in ads belong to `mobile-app-growth`. This agent supplies the term map, the CPP and organic conversion data.

## 8. Reading search performance in App Store Connect

| Metric or view | Use | Note |
|----------------|-----|------|
| Impressions, product page views, first-time downloads by source type (App Store search, App Store browse, app referrer, web referrer) | Store funnel by source | Compare the same source across periods |
| Conversion rate | App Store Connect's own definition (check the tooltip); never mix with a ratio you compute differently | Peer group benchmarks exist for comparison [Official] |
| Top web referrers and app referrers | AI referrals arrive here when users tap a store link in chatgpt.com, Perplexity, Gemini or their apps | Treat as an AI referral source; links with `ct` values make it explicit |
| Campaign links (`pt` provider token, `ct` campaign up to 30 characters) | Attribute web and partner traffic, including AI answer surfaces you control | [Official] for the parameters; spec in [App web presence for AI](app-web-presence-for-ai.md) |
| Custom product page metrics | Per page impressions, conversion, downloads | Keyword linked pages read separately |
| In-App Purchase and subscription analytics (2026-03) | Downstream quality by source | [Official, 2026-03] (moved) |

Weekly read (15 minutes): conversion by source vs the prior 4 weeks, ranks for the term map, impressions from search, new ratings and average, tag set, CPP and PPO status, competitor metadata changes from the ASO tool.

## 9. Expensive mistakes

1. Changing name, subtitle, keywords, icon and screenshots in one release: nothing can be attributed.
2. Competitor names in any indexed field: rejection or takedown.
3. Repeating words across name, subtitle and keyword field.
4. Translating keywords literally instead of researching each locale ([Localization](localization-and-market-expansion.md)).
5. Judging a metadata change on day 3.
6. Captions written for OCR instead of for people.
7. Stuffing the description as if it were indexed.
8. Deselecting tags to "clean up" without a reason, or deselecting all of them.
9. Building keyword linked custom product pages for terms that are not in the keyword space, then wondering why they never appear.
