## Academic classics (prior knowledge, no URL captured)

- Marn and Rosiello (1992), "Managing Price, Gaining Profit", Harvard Business Review: 1% price, about 11% operating profit.
- Anderson and Simester (2003), "Effects of $9 Price Endings on Retail Sales", Quantitative Marketing and Economics.
- Bijmolt, van Heerde and Pieters (2005), "New Empirical Generalizations on the Determinants of Price Elasticity", Journal of Marketing Research: mean about -2.6.
- Lewis, Singh and Fay (2006), nonlinear shipping fees, Marketing Science.
- Huber, Payne and Puto (1982) decoy effect; Frederick, Lee and Baskin (2014) and Yang and Lynn (2014) replication failures.
- Scheibehenne, Greifeneder and Todd (2010) choice overload meta-analysis; Chernev, Böckenholt and Goodman (2015).
- Wadhwa and Zhang (2015), round vs non-round prices, Journal of Consumer Research.
- Directive 98/6/EC (price indication), Directive 2019/2161 (Omnibus), Regulation 2022/720 (VBER).
