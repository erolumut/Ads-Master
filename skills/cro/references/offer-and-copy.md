# Offer Architecture, Value Proposition and Copy

> The offer and the value proposition decide most of the conversion rate. Layout tweaks move it at the margin. Fix offer and clarity first.

## 1. Offer architecture

An offer is everything the visitor gets and gives. Write it down as a table before writing copy.

| Component | Question | Levers |
|-----------|----------|--------|
| Core product or service | What exactly do they get? | Configuration, tiers, bundles, done-for-you vs self-serve |
| Price and payment terms | What do they pay and when? | Bundle pricing, subscription discount, installments, BNPL, annual plan |
| Bonus | What extra makes it a better deal now? | Free accessory, onboarding, template pack, consultation |
| Risk reversal | What happens if it does not work? | Money back, free returns, trial, cancel anytime, results guarantee |
| Urgency or scarcity (real only) | Why now? | Real deadline, real stock, shipping cutoff, seasonal, cohort start date |
| Effort and time to value | How fast and how easy? | Setup included, delivery speed, "live in 1 day" |
| Proof | Why believe it? | Reviews, case studies, data, credentials |

Value equation (Alex Hormozi) as a checklist [Practitioner]: value rises with dream outcome and perceived likelihood of achievement, and falls with time delay and effort. Every copy block should raise one of the first two or lower one of the last two.

### Offer tests worth more than design tests
| Business model | Offer tests to run first |
|----------------|--------------------------|
| Ecommerce | Bundle vs single, free shipping threshold, subscribe and save rate, gift with purchase vs percent off, guarantee length |
| Lead gen | Free quote vs free inspection vs price calculator, consultation length, response time promise |
| B2B SaaS | Free trial vs freemium vs reverse trial, card required or not, demo vs instant access, annual discount size |
| Local services | Fixed price vs range, same day slot, satisfaction guarantee |
| App | Trial length, paywall placement, introductory price |
| Marketplace | Buyer protection, first order credit |

Discount claims in the EU must reference the lowest price applied in the prior 30 days (Price Indication Directive as amended by the Omnibus Directive, applied from May 2022) [Official, 2022-05]. Hand pricing changes that affect margin to `growth-orchestrator`.

## 2. Value proposition

A value proposition answers: what it is, who it is for, what outcome it delivers, and why it is better than the alternative.

Structure for the hero:
- Headline: the outcome or the offer, specific.
- Subheadline: who it is for and how it works, 1 to 2 sentences.
- 3 bullets: the top benefits or differentiators, each with a number or proof.
- Visual: product in use or the result.

Positioning statement from `ads-master/BRAND.md`: "For (who) who (need), we are the (category) that (key benefit), unlike (alternative)." The hero must be derivable from that line.

### Specificity test
Replace adjectives with numbers, names and mechanisms.
| Weak | Specific |
|------|----------|
| Fast delivery | Order by 3 pm, delivered tomorrow (UK mainland) |
| Trusted by thousands | 41,212 orders shipped in 2026, 4.8 stars from 6,300 reviews |
| Powerful analytics | See which ad drove each sale, synced every 15 minutes |
| Expert team | Installed by Gas Safe registered engineers, 12 years average experience |

Only use numbers you can substantiate from PROJECT_BRIEF.md, BRAND.md or data. If a number is not available, ask; never invent one.

## 3. Headlines

Formulas (choose by awareness level):
| Formula | Use for | Example |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Outcome + timeframe | Solution aware | "Fix your posture in 10 minutes a day" |
| Outcome + without objection | Solution aware with known objection | "Clear skin without harsh retinoids" |
| For [who] + outcome | Segmented B2B and paid social | "Payroll for restaurants that runs itself" |
| Offer led | Most aware | "Get 2 months free on annual plans" |
| Question in their words | Problem aware | "Still waking up with back pain?" |
| Comparison | Product aware | "The 4K camera that costs half a GoPro" |
| Query echo | Search and ChatGPT ads | "Running shoes for flat feet" |

Generate 10 headlines per page from VOC phrases, then pick 3 to test. Score each on: clarity (a stranger understands in 5 s), specificity, match to the ad, believability.

## 4. Copy frameworks

| Framework | Structure | Best for |
|-----------|-----------|----------|
| PAS | Problem, Agitate, Solution | Problem aware cold traffic, short pages |
| AIDA | Attention, Interest, Desire, Action | General long form |
| BAB | Before, After, Bridge | Transformation offers, testimonials |
| 4Ps | Promise, Picture, Proof, Push | Direct response LPs |
| FAB | Feature, Advantage, Benefit | Feature sections, PDP bullets |
| StoryBrand (SB7) | Character, Problem, Guide, Plan, Call to action, Avoid failure, Success | Service businesses, homepages |
| PASTOR | Problem, Amplify, Story, Transformation, Offer, Response | Long sales letters, high price consumer offers |

Rules:
- One idea per section. One reader. Second person.
- Lead with the customer's words (from VOC), not the company's words.
- Each feature gets a "so that" clause.
- Grade 5 to 8 reading level for consumer pages, grade 8 to 10 for B2B [Practitioner consensus]. Check with a readability tool.
- Sentences under 20 words on average. Paragraphs under 3 lines on mobile.
- Specific beats clever. Clever headlines lose more tests than they win [Practitioner consensus].

## 5. VOC-led copywriting procedure (message mining)
1. Pull 200 to 500 verbatims (reviews, surveys, tickets, calls, Reddit). See [Research methods](research-methods.md).
2. Code into pains, desired outcomes, objections, triggers, alternatives.
3. Pick the top 3 pains and top 3 outcomes by frequency in the target segment.
4. Write the hero from the top outcome, the problem section from the top pains, the FAQ from the top objections.
5. Mark each line in the copy deck with its source quote ID. If a line has no source, it is an assumption: label it.

## 6. CTA copy
- Verb + outcome: "Get my plan", "See pricing", "Book my install".
- First person ("my") vs second person ("your") is a cheap test; results vary [Contested].
- State commitment and reduce anxiety under the button: "No card required", "Takes 2 minutes", "Cancel anytime".
- Avoid "Submit", "Click here", "Learn more" as primary CTA.

## 7. Risk reversal

| Type | When | Copy pattern |
|------|------|--------------|
| Money back guarantee | Consumer products, courses, software | "Try it for 60 days. If you do not love it, send it back for a full refund. Return shipping on us." |
| Free returns and exchanges | Apparel, footwear | "Free returns within 30 days, no questions" |
| Free trial | SaaS, subscriptions | "Free for 14 days. No card required." |
| Cancel anytime | Subscriptions | "Skip, pause or cancel online in two clicks" |
| Results guarantee | Services with controllable outcomes | "If we do not get you X in Y days, the next month is free" (only if legally and operationally real) |
| Price match | Commodity retail | "Found it cheaper? We match it" |
| Fixed price | Local services | "The price we quote is the price you pay" |

Place risk reversal under the primary CTA, next to the price, in the cart and in checkout. A guarantee only in the footer does nothing.

Cancellation: the US FTC "click to cancel" negative option rule was vacated by the Eighth Circuit on procedural grounds in July 2025; the FTC reopened rulemaking with an advance notice in March 2026 (comments closed 2026-04-13) and keeps enforcing ROSCA and Section 5 meanwhile [Official, 2025-07 and 2026-03], but state laws (for example California automatic renewal law) and EU consumer law still require easy cancellation. Make cancellation as easy as signup regardless.

## 8. Social proof and trust

### 8.1 Types, ranked by persuasive strength for cold traffic [Practitioner consensus]
1. Specific results from similar customers (name, role or location, photo, number).
2. Volume of reviews with average rating from a third party platform.
3. Recognizable customer logos (B2B) or press logos.
4. Expert or professional endorsements (with disclosure of any payment).
5. Social following counts (weakest).

### 8.2 Legal limits (do not cross)
| Jurisdiction | Rule | Implication |
|-------------|------|-------------|
| US FTC | Rule on the use of consumer reviews and testimonials, effective 21 October 2024 [Official, 2024-08]: bans fake or AI-generated fake reviews, buying reviews, review suppression, undisclosed insider reviews, fake social media indicators | Never generate testimonials. Never hide negative reviews. Disclose incentives. |
| US FTC | Endorsement Guides (2023 update) | Material connections must be disclosed; results claims must be typical or qualified |
| EU | Unfair Commercial Practices Directive as amended by the Omnibus Directive (applied May 2022) [Official]: must state whether and how reviews are verified; fake reviews banned | Add "How we verify reviews" note near reviews |
| UK | Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024, consumer enforcement from April 2025 [Unverified exact date, verify]: fake reviews and drip pricing banned, CMA can fine directly | Show total price upfront; no fake reviews |

### 8.3 Trust signal inventory (check what exists, show what is real)
Third party rating badges, payment logos, security (HTTPS is baseline; "secure checkout" near payment fields), physical address and phone, real team photos, years in business, certifications and memberships, return and privacy policies linked near the ask, delivery partners. Baymard's checkout research lists "did not trust the site with my credit card information" as a frequent abandonment reason (about 19% to 25% of abandoners depending on denominator) [Study, 2024 to 2025].

## 9. Urgency and scarcity without dark patterns

Allowed when true and verifiable:
- Real deadline: sale ends on a stated date and time, and the price actually changes after.
- Real stock: inventory from the platform ("Only 3 left in size 9") updated live.
- Shipping cutoff: "Order within 2 h 14 min for delivery Thursday" from real carrier cutoffs.
- Cohort or seasonal limits: "Next cohort starts 4 November, 40 seats".

Not allowed (illegal in many markets, and they erode trust):
| Pattern | Why it is banned or risky |
|---------|---------------------------|
| Countdown timers that reset per visitor or session | EU Unfair Commercial Practices Directive Annex I (falsely stating limited availability) [Official]; FTC deception |
| Fake "X people are viewing this" or fake recent purchase popups | Fake social proof under FTC reviews rule and EU UCPD |
| Pre-ticked add-ons or insurance | EU Consumer Rights Directive Article 22 requires express consent for extra payments [Official] |
| Drip pricing (fees revealed at checkout) | FTC junk fees rule for live events and short-term lodging (effective May 2025) [Unverified date], California SB 478 (July 2024), UK DMCC Act; also a top abandonment cause in Baymard data |
| Confirmshaming ("No thanks, I like paying more") | EU Digital Services Act Article 25 bans manipulative interface design on online platforms [Official, applies since Feb 2024]; erodes brand |
| Hard-to-cancel subscriptions | State automatic renewal laws, EU rules; the EU Digital Fairness Act targets dark patterns and unfair personalization; the Commission work programme schedules the proposal for Q4 2026 and the Commissioner said in May 2026 it will come before year end; not proposed as of early October 2026, legal form (regulation or directive) open [Official, 2026-05; check Commission press releases] |
| Hidden opt-outs for marketing consent | GDPR requires freely given, unambiguous consent |

## 10. Pricing presentation
- Anchor: show the higher reference first (bundle value, competitor cost, cost of the problem).
- Break down: per day, per use, per seat; only when the unit is meaningful.
- Free shipping threshold: set from AOV data, commonly 10% to 30% above current AOV [Practitioner consensus]; test, and watch margin.
- Show savings in both currency and percent when the discount is large; currency for expensive items, percent for cheap items [Practitioner consensus].
- Payment options near price: installments (Klarna, Afterpay, PayPal Pay Later, Shop Pay Installments) show "4 payments of $24.75". Local regulation applies to credit promotion wording.

## 11. Claims compliance
- Use only approved claims in `ads-master/BRAND.md`. Anything new goes to the human as a proposed claim with the evidence needed.
- Regulated categories (health, finance, legal, alcohol, gambling, housing, employment, credit): no outcome promises, required disclaimers, check platform ad policies; landing pages are reviewed by Google, Meta, OpenAI and others.
- Before and after images: allowed only where policy and law allow, with typical result disclosures.
- Comparative claims: must be accurate and verifiable; name the comparison basis and date.
