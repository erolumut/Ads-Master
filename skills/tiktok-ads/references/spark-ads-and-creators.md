# Spark Ads and Creators

> Knowledge as of 2026-10. Covers Spark Ads mechanics, authorization, TikTok One (which replaced Creator Marketplace in 2025), creator sourcing and contracts, affiliate creators for TikTok Shop, Custom Creator Networks, and disclosure. Creator content is the single biggest performance lever on TikTok.

## 1. Spark Ads vs non-Spark ads

| Dimension | Spark Ads | Non-Spark (custom identity) |
|-----------|-----------|-----------------------------|
| Source | An existing organic post from your TikTok account or an authorized creator's account | Video uploaded to Ads Manager |
| Identity shown | The real account (brand or creator) with profile link | Display name and avatar you set |
| Engagement | Likes, comments, shares and follows accrue to the organic post and account | Engagement stays on the ad |
| Social proof | Can show the post's existing engagement | Starts from zero |
| Control | Post must stay live; creator can revoke or delete; caption is the organic caption | Full control of text and lifecycle |
| Comments | Real public comments on the post; must be moderated | Comments can be managed or disabled per ad settings |
| Best for | Creator and organic winners, social proof, community growth, Shop | Pure performance variants, many hook edits, regulated copy that must be locked |

Default: run winning creator and organic content as Spark Ads; use non-Spark for rapid hook variations of the same footage. Test both on the same footage when stakes are high. TikTok has published internal claims that Spark Ads outperform non-Spark on engagement and conversion rates [Unverified figures; ask your rep for current data].

## 2. Authorization workflow

1. Creator (or brand account) opens the post's settings and turns on ad authorization, choosing a duration of 7, 30, 60 or 365 days [Official, Help Center "How to create Spark Ads for Smart+ Campaigns"; 365 days added later per Lindsey Gamble]. When the code expires, ads using it stop and the code cannot be reactivated; request a new code before expiry, then generates the video code.
2. The code is sent to the advertiser, who adds it in Ads Manager (Creative or Assets > TikTok Posts > Apply for authorization or Add by code).
3. Alternatively, request authorization through TikTok One or the account-linking flow so posts can be selected without manual codes.
4. Record each code in the creator register: post URL, creator, code, start date, expiry, usage terms, fee.
5. Set a reminder 7 days before expiry. Expired authorization stops the ad.

Brand account linking: link the brand's own TikTok account to Ads Manager (Business Center) so every organic post is available as a Spark Ad without codes.

## 4. Creator sourcing playbook

| Tier | Sourcing approach | Volume target |
|------|------------------|---------------|
| Starter | Brand's own account plus 1 to 2 micro creators (product-for-content or small fee); TikTok Shop affiliates if Shop exists | 2 to 4 creator videos per month |
| Growth | TikTok One search + direct outreach; 3 to 6 creators on monthly retainers; UGC platforms | 8 to 20 videos per month |
| Scale | Creator roster of 15 to 50, tiered (whitelisting partners, UGC producers, affiliates); TikTok One campaigns; agency or in-house creator manager | 30 to 100 videos per month |
| Enterprise | Custom Creator Networks where offered, Branded Mission, Branded Buzz, market-level rosters | 100+ per month across markets |

Selection criteria (score 1 to 5 each): on-camera clarity, audience fit (comment section demographics), past branded content performance, delivery reliability, content rights flexibility, brand safety history. Reject creators with recent controversies; review the last 50 posts.

Creator types:
- UGC producers: make ads that run on the brand's account or as non-Spark. Cheap, fast, no audience.
- Whitelisting (Spark) creators: post on their account; brand runs paid behind it. Creator's identity adds trust.
- Affiliates (TikTok Shop): earn commission per sale; GMV Max can use their content automatically.
- Experts and professionals: needed for health-adjacent, finance and B2B claims; verify credentials.

## 5. Contract and usage terms checklist

```
- Deliverables: number of videos, hooks, raw footage, revisions
- Posting: on creator account? date window? caption approval?
- Spark Ads authorization: duration (7, 30, 60 or 365 days in the TikTok app; contract for 60 or 365), renewal fee
- Paid usage outside TikTok (Meta, YouTube): term and territories
- Exclusivity: category and term (price it explicitly)
- Edit rights: brand may cut, re-hook, caption, dub, translate
- AI rights: brand may or may not create AI variants, voice clones or avatars (default: NO without separate written consent)
- Disclosure: creator enables branded content / paid partnership disclosure on every sponsored post
- Claims: creator uses only approved claims; no medical, financial or earnings claims
- Takedown: brand may request removal; creator may not delete during the Spark term
- Payment: fee, commission (affiliate %), bonuses for performance thresholds
- Morality and brand safety clause
```

## 6. Disclosure and branded content rules
- Creators must turn on the content disclosure setting (branded content / paid partnership) for any post made in exchange for payment, free product or commission [Official, Branded Content Policy].
- Spark Ads from creator posts show an ad label in addition to the creator's disclosure.
- AI-generated or AI-altered realistic content must carry the AI label. Do not create AI likenesses of a creator without a separate written license.
- Regulators (FTC in the US, ASA in the UK, consumer authorities in the EU) treat undisclosed paid endorsements as deceptive. Hand legal questions to the human.

## 7. Affiliate creators for TikTok Shop

| Mechanism | What it does |
|-----------|-------------|
| Open collaboration | Any eligible affiliate can promote the product for a set commission |
| Targeted collaboration | Invite specific creators with a custom commission |
| Free samples | Send product to vetted creators; track content delivery rate |
| Affiliate content in GMV Max | Product GMV Max can use own videos, Spark-authorized posts and affiliate content [Official] |
| Cost visibility | From 2026-05, GMV Max reporting and GMV Max Pro optimization factor affiliate commissions, coupons and platform fees [Official, 2026-05] |

Affiliate program rules:
1. Set commission by margin: commission + referral fee + shipping subsidy + ad cost must leave contribution margin positive at target ROI.
2. Track sample-to-post rate and post-to-sale rate per creator; stop sampling creators below 30% post rate after two shipments [Practitioner consensus].
3. Identify affiliate videos with strong organic sales and request Spark authorization or boost through GMV Max creative controls where available.
4. Watch double payment: a sale may carry both affiliate commission and ad cost. Model it in the ROI target.

## 8. Comment and community management for Spark Ads
- Check comments on every Spark Ad daily at Growth tier and above. Pin a helpful comment (FAQ, sizing, shipping).
- Reply to objections with video replies; these make new organic posts and Spark candidates.
- Hide or report spam and abusive comments; do not delete legitimate criticism (it is visible and deletion invites backlash).
- If a creator becomes controversial, pause their Spark Ads within hours and log in the journal.

## 9. Creator performance register (columns)

```
creator_handle | tier | content_type | post_url | spark_code | code_expiry | fee | commission_% |
spend | impressions | hook_rate | 6s_rate | ctr | cpa | roas | gmv (shop) | status | notes
```

Rank creators monthly by CPA or ROI at comparable spend. Renew the top quartile, re-brief the middle, drop the bottom quartile.

## 10. Spark Ads launch procedure
1. Pick candidates: organic posts (brand or creator) in the top quartile of the account for 6s view rate and shares in the last 30 days, with on-brand comments.
2. Check rights: Spark authorization valid for the planned flight, contract covers paid usage, disclosure on.
3. Clean the post: pin a helpful comment, remove spam, confirm the caption has no prohibited claims (the organic caption becomes ad text).
4. Add as a new ad in the testing lane (or directly into a Smart+ asset group at Scale tier) with the same CTA and URL as current winners.
5. Read after 3 to 5 days with the kill/keep/scale rules.
6. For winners, request 3 hook variations from the same creator and a longer authorization.

## 12. Pricing approach (no market averages quoted)
Creator rates vary widely by niche, market, deliverables and usage rights, and no verified 2026 rate benchmark was found in this research cycle. Price with:
1. A base fee per video that the brand would pay a UGC producer for the same deliverable.
2. A usage fee that scales with Spark duration and paid usage outside TikTok.
3. A performance component (bonus at CPA or GMV thresholds, or affiliate commission) to align incentives.
4. A cap on total cost per video equal to the expected contribution from the sales it must drive at target CPA.
