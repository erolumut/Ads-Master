# Formats and Creative

> Scope: every LinkedIn ad format with when to use it, specifications, Thought Leader Ads, Reserved and First Impression Ads, BrandLink and CTV, AI creative tools, creative principles for B2B, angle library, testing and fatigue control. Verified 2026-10-08 where labeled; specifications change, so confirm in the LinkedIn Ads Guide before production. Values marked [Unverified] could not be confirmed in an official source in that pass.

## 3. Thought Leader Ads
- What: promote public posts from members as ads from your Page: employees, creators, and members who are 1st, 2nd or 3rd degree connections, after they approve. Expansion beyond employees was announced 2024-03-13 and rolled out globally by end of 2024-03 [Official, 2024-03]. Sponsoring member posts that link to a LinkedIn Event was added in 2025 [Unverified] for the month.
- Eligible posts: public text, single image, video, article and newsletter posts, and eligible collaborative posts [Official, 2026-10].
- Objectives: Brand awareness and Engagement are confirmed on LinkedIn's product page; the Help Center lists Video views for video posts; Lead generation with a lead gen form is [Contested] (LinkedIn's product page omits it, while Kiin's 2026 dataset and an agency guide report lead gen forms on Thought Leader Ads in feed). Check the objective menu before planning a lead gen Thought Leader program.
- Limits: the advertiser cannot edit the post text, add a headline or a CTA button on image and video posts; dynamic UTMs do not apply [Practitioner consensus, 2026].
- Permissions: the member approves in the Campaign Manager flow; keep a record of approvals; never promote without consent.
- Creator Marketplace (alpha since 2026-06-10, North America, English only): search creators by topic, see follower job titles and industries, and promote their posts as Thought Leader Ads from Campaign Manager; BrandWorks is LinkedIn's managed creative service for select managed customers globally [Official, 2026-06].
- Evidence on performance:
  - Kiin, 212 accounts, 2025-09 to 2026-09: median landing page CTR 0.46% and median CPM $65. In paired accounts Thought Leader Ads cost $74.71 CPM vs $48.80 for single image with nearly identical landing page CTR (0.449% vs 0.441%): the premium buys engagement and dwell, not cheaper clicks [Study, 2026-09].
  - ZenABM (161,256 ads): median CPC $2.29 for Thought Leader Ads vs $13.23 for single image, but these clicks include social engagement [Study, 2026].
  - Lead gen forms (Kiin, 489 advertisers): content offers on Thought Leader Ads completed at 11.4% and $94 per lead vs 4.6% and $119 on video; demo offers cost $1,025 per lead on Thought Leader Ads vs $283 on video (small cells, 8 to 16 accounts) [Study, 2026].
  - Implication: use Thought Leader Ads for reach, engagement and engager pools; do not expect cheaper landing page traffic or demo leads.
- Playbook:
  1. Identify 2 to 5 credible voices (founder, product lead, customer, analyst, paid creator).
  2. Publish organically first; promote the posts that earn above average organic engagement.
  3. Target ICP cold audiences with Brand awareness or Engagement; build engager retargeting pools.
  4. Retarget engagers with proof and conversion offers from company page ads.
  5. Report on cost per engagement, landing page CTR where links exist, engager pool size and later pipeline from engagers.

## 4. Video, Reserved placements, BrandLink and CTV
- Video in feed: the workhorse for demand creation; build view based retargeting (50% and 75% viewers). Funnel reports video warmed members are 1.6x as likely to open a lead gen form in a 30 day window [Unverified].
- Reserved Ads and First Impression Ads: see the format map; use for launches, events and category moments at Enterprise tier, measured on ICP reach and lift.
- BrandLink: started as the Wire program (publisher pre-roll beta 2024-06, EU publishers 2024-10), renamed BrandLink around 2025-05 and opened to creator video in the US and UK; LinkedIn reports 130% higher completion and 23% higher view rate than standard video ads (platform claim) [Official, 2025-05]. Since 2026-03 BrandLink campaigns with publishers are self serve in Campaign Manager for select customers; LinkedIn's BrandLink page still calls it a beta and routes advertisers to sales [Official, 2026-03]. Creators receive a revenue share (100+ creators reported) [Unverified].
- In-Stream Ads: reported as an alpha in the US for pre-roll against vetted publisher and creator content (2026-09 recap) [Unverified].
- CTV Ads timeline [Official, 2026-05]:
  - 2024-04: CTV Ads launched at B2Believe for US and Canadian advertisers with Roku and Samsung Ads inventory, plus LinkedIn Premiere with NBCUniversal; iSpot named as measurement partner 2024-06.
  - 2025-06-04: Premiere renamed CTV Select with Paramount inventory; VAST tags via Innovid and a CTV API (Sprinklr first).
  - 2026-03: programmatic buying through The Trade Desk, with Microsoft Monetize as exclusive supply.
  - 2026-05: LinkedIn CTV Ads available through Amazon DSP in the US via deals, using LinkedIn job title, industry and seniority signals.
  - Microsoft Advertising CTV does not offer LinkedIn profile targeting (Microsoft clarification, 2026-05).
- CTV evidence: LinkedIn reports its CTV ads were 2.2x more effective than other CTV platforms and 4.3x more than linear TV (own research) [Official, 2026-03 platform claim]; Kiin's 31 account panel shows a median CTV CPM of $25.76 [Study, 2026]. Markets: US and Canada confirmed; UK reported by one source [Contested].
- Frequency: Brand awareness frequency caps apply across feed, Audience Network and CTV (3 to 30 impressions per member per 7 days) [Official, 2025-07].
- Measurement: video and CTV are judged on reach in the ICP, completion rate, brand lift and later pipeline influence; plan a lift study if spend is material. DoubleVerify measures LinkedIn CTV Ads [Official, 2026-05].
- Creative rules: first 3 seconds carry the hook; captions on; brand in the first 5 seconds; 15 to 45 seconds for cold audiences; longer for retargeting and webinars.

### AI creative tools in Campaign Manager (self serve from 2026-07-01) [Official, 2026-07]
| Tool | What it does | How to use it safely |
|------|-------------|----------------------|
| Brand Kit | Stores colors, fonts, logo, tone of voice and key messages, prefilled from the Page | Load BRAND.md rules first; review the generated voice |
| Draft with AI | Builds a single image ad draft (intro text, headline, media, CTA, alt text) from a URL and goals | Treat as a first draft; check every claim against sourced facts |
| AI ad variants | Generates 3 variations of an existing ad's intro text and headline in the same ad set | LinkedIn claims 5+ variants lift CTR over 20% vs 1 ad (platform data, no method) [Official, 2026-07]; still screen on qualified results |
| Ad personalization | Inserts profile fields (first name, job title, industry, company) into Sponsored Content copy via a "Use ad personalization" toggle | Test against non personalized copy; avoid creepy phrasing; respect member ad controls |
| Flexible Ad Creation | Mixes up to 4 images or videos with up to 5 headlines and descriptions into up to 64 versions | Needs a new campaign with dynamic budget optimization; about one week learning; auto pauses weak combinations |

## 5. Creative principles for B2B on LinkedIn
1. Lead with the problem in the buyer's words, not the product name.
2. Specific beats clever: numbers, named outcomes, named roles.
3. One idea per ad; one CTA.
4. Proof early: customer logos (with permission), quantified results, analyst mentions.
5. Native look outperforms polished brand ads in feed for many B2B audiences [Practitioner consensus].
6. Match the offer to the stage: cold audiences get value (insight, tool, report), warm audiences get proof, hot audiences get the meeting.
7. Write for the role: CFO angle differs from practitioner angle.
8. Mobile first: most feed consumption is mobile [Practitioner consensus]; check text cutoffs.

| Angle | Example hook pattern |
|-------|---------------------|

## 7. Testing and fatigue

Testing standard:
- Use Campaign Manager A/B testing where available, or duplicate campaigns with identical settings and split budget.
- Test one variable: angle, format, offer, or audience. Not several at once.
- Minimum: 2 weeks and enough results to compare (aim for 30+ results per arm for lead tests; use engagement metrics for early creative screening).
- Primary metric: cost per qualified lead or downstream metric when volume allows; CTR and engagement rate for early screening only.

Fatigue signals and actions:
| Signal | Threshold | Action |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| CTR down from first 2 weeks | 30%+ decline | Refresh creative or angle |
| Frequency (30 days) in small audiences | Rising while CTR falls | Add new ads, widen audience, lower budget |
| Cost per result up with stable CPM | 25%+ | Creative or offer issue |
| Negative comments or hides | Any pattern | Review message and targeting |

Refresh cadence: every 4 to 8 weeks for small audiences and ABM, 8 to 12 weeks for broad audiences [Practitioner consensus]. For Brand awareness ad sets, a Customize frequency cap (for example 4 to 6 per 7 days in ABM tiers) limits fatigue mechanically [Official, 2025-07].

## 8. Copy templates (fill with sourced facts only)
Single image or document intro text (keep the hook within the first 150 characters):
```
<Role>s: <specific problem in their words>.
We analyzed <data source> and found <specific finding>.
<Offer>: <what they get> in <time>.
```
Conversation ad opening message:
```
Hi %FIRSTNAME%, I lead <function> at <company>. Teams like yours tell us <pain>.
Would one of these help?
[See the 6 minute demo] [Get the benchmark report] [Talk to a specialist]
```
Thought Leader post prompt (for the member to write in their own voice):
```
A lesson from <experience>, the mistake most <role>s make, what we do instead, one question for readers.
```
Check personalization macros and character limits in the current Ads Guide [Unverified] for Sponsored Messaging macros; Sponsored Content uses the Ad personalization toggle [Official, 2026-07].
