# Formats and Creative

> Scope: every LinkedIn ad format with when to use it, specifications to verify, Thought Leader Ads, BrandLink and CTV, creative principles for B2B, angle library, testing and fatigue control. Specifications change; confirm in the LinkedIn Ads Guide before production. Values below marked [Unverified] are as known to the author up to 2025.

## 1. Format map

| Format | Family | Objectives (typical) | Best use | Watch out |
|--------|--------|----------------------|----------|-----------|
| Single image | Sponsored Content | All | Offers, proof, retargeting, lead gen | Fatigue in small audiences |
| Carousel | Sponsored Content | Awareness, consideration, leads | Step by step stories, multiple products or use cases | Higher production cost |
| Video | Sponsored Content | Awareness, video views, consideration, leads | Demand creation, demos, customer stories | Judge on views and later pipeline, not same day leads |
| Document | Sponsored Content | Awareness, consideration, lead generation | Native reports, checklists, playbooks; optional gating with a lead form | Gate too early and volume falls |
| Event | Sponsored Content | Awareness, engagement, registrations | Webinars, LinkedIn Live, in person events | Needs a LinkedIn Event page |
| Thought Leader Ads | Sponsored Content (member post) | Awareness, engagement, video views, website visits and more [Unverified] | Executive and expert posts promoted to the ICP | Needs member permission per post |
| Conversation ads | Sponsored Messaging | Lead generation, website visits | Choose your own path offers (demo, content, event) | Regional delivery limits; sender credibility |
| Message ads | Sponsored Messaging | Lead generation, website visits | Single direct invitation | Regional delivery limits; low tolerance for generic copy |
| Text ads | Desktop right rail | Website visits, leads | Cheap retargeting presence | Small, desktop only |
| Dynamic ads (Follower, Spotlight) | Personalized | Awareness, engagement | Page follower growth, personalized offers | Limited creative control |
| Click to message | Sponsored Content | Engagement, leads [Unverified] | Starting a conversation from a feed ad | Needs inbox follow up |
| Connected TV and BrandLink | Video | Awareness, video views | Reach professionals on CTV and alongside publisher or creator video [Unverified] for availability | Premium CPMs, measurement by lift |

## 3. Thought Leader Ads
- What: promote posts from members (employees and, since 2024, other members such as customers, partners or creators who approve) as ads from your account [Official, 2024].
- Why: B2B buyers engage with people; practitioners report higher engagement and lower cost per engagement than company page posts [Practitioner consensus]. LinkedIn has published comparisons favoring Thought Leader Ads [Unverified] for figures.
- Permissions: the member approves in the Campaign Manager flow; keep a record of approvals; never promote without consent.
- Formats supported: text, image, video and document posts have been supported at various times; check current support and objectives [Unverified].
- Playbook:
  1. Identify 2 to 5 credible voices (founder, product lead, customer, analyst).
  2. Publish organically first; promote the posts that earn above average organic engagement.
  3. Target ICP cold audiences with Brand awareness or Engagement; build engager retargeting pools.
  4. Retarget engagers with proof and conversion offers from company page ads.
  5. Report on cost per engagement, follower growth of the leader, engager pool size and later pipeline from engagers.

## 4. Video, BrandLink and CTV
- Video in feed: the workhorse for demand creation; build view based retargeting (50% and 75% viewers).
- BrandLink: LinkedIn program that lets brands run ads with premium publisher and creator video content on LinkedIn, launched 2024 and expanded later [Unverified] for 2026 terms and markets.
- CTV: LinkedIn has offered reaching its audiences on connected TV inventory [Unverified] for 2026 availability, partners and minimums.
- Measurement: video and CTV are judged on reach in the ICP, completion rate, brand lift and later pipeline influence; plan a lift study if spend is material.
- Creative rules: first 3 seconds carry the hook; captions on; brand in the first 5 seconds; 15 to 45 seconds for cold audiences; longer for retargeting and webinars.

## 5. Creative principles for B2B on LinkedIn
1. Lead with the problem in the buyer's words, not the product name.
2. Specific beats clever: numbers, named outcomes, named roles.
3. One idea per ad; one CTA.
4. Proof early: customer logos (with permission), quantified results, analyst mentions.
5. Native look outperforms polished brand ads in feed for many B2B audiences [Practitioner consensus].
6. Match the offer to the stage: cold audiences get value (insight, tool, report), warm audiences get proof, hot audiences get the meeting.
7. Write for the role: CFO angle differs from practitioner angle.
8. Mobile first: most feed consumption is mobile [Practitioner consensus]; check text cutoffs.

### Angle library (rotate across campaigns)
| Angle | Example hook pattern |
|-------|---------------------|
| Cost of inaction | "Every month without X costs a 200 person team about Y" (use your data) |
| Benchmark | "How your <metric> compares with 300 <industry> teams" |
| Myth vs reality | "Most <role>s think <belief>. The data says otherwise." |
| Framework | "The 5 step checklist we use before every <process>" |
| Customer story | "<Customer> cut <metric> by <result> in <time>" |
| Contrarian | "Stop doing <common practice>. Do this instead." |
| Event or timing | "New <regulation or change> takes effect <date>. Here is what to do." |
| Role specific pain | "If you own <KPI>, this is for you." |

## 6. Offer ladder
| Stage | Offer | Format | Objective |
|-------|-------|--------|-----------|
| Cold | Ungated insight (document, video, Thought Leader post) | Document, video, TLA | Awareness, Engagement, Video views |
| Warm | Gated report, benchmark, template, webinar | Document with lead form, event ads, single image with lead form | Lead generation |
| Hot | Demo, assessment, trial, pricing call | Conversation ads, single image, message ads | Lead generation, Website conversions |
| Customer | Upsell, community, advocacy | Single image, event | Engagement, Website visits |

## 7. Testing and fatigue

Testing standard:
- Use Campaign Manager A/B testing where available, or duplicate campaigns with identical settings and split budget.
- Test one variable: angle, format, offer, or audience. Not several at once.
- Minimum: 2 weeks and enough results to compare (aim for 30+ results per arm for lead tests; use engagement metrics for early creative screening).
- Primary metric: cost per qualified lead or downstream metric when volume allows; CTR and engagement rate for early screening only.

Fatigue signals and actions:
| Signal | Threshold | Action |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| CTR down from first 2 weeks | 30%+ decline | Refresh creative or angle |
| Frequency (30 days) in small audiences | Rising while CTR falls | Add new ads, widen audience, lower budget |
| Cost per result up with stable CPM | 25%+ | Creative or offer issue |
| Negative comments or hides | Any pattern | Review message and targeting |

Refresh cadence: every 4 to 8 weeks for small audiences and ABM, 8 to 12 weeks for broad audiences [Practitioner consensus].
