# Ad Video Grammar

> Purpose: the craft rules that make a video work as a performance ad: the first 2 seconds, structure by length, pacing, captions, sound, branding, end cards and the native grammar of each platform. `creative-strategy` owns the angle and the hook ideas; this module turns them into frames and seconds.

## 1. Evidence base (read the caveats)

| Finding | Source and date | Caveat |
|---------|-----------------|--------|
| TikTok recommends introducing the core proposition in the first 3 seconds and using text overlays for context | TikTok Ads Help Center, creative best practices for performance ads [Official, current 2026] | Guidance, not a controlled study |
| 63 percent of TikTok videos with the highest CTR highlight the key message or product in the first 3 seconds | TikTok internal research cited in a 2022 partner guide [Study, 2022, secondary] | Old, internal, correlational |

## 2. The first 2 seconds

Three jobs, all in the first 1 to 2 seconds:

1. **Stop**: a visual or sonic pattern break (motion in frame 1, unusual scale, a face addressing the viewer, a sound that is not music bed).
2. **Signal**: who this is for or what problem it is about (the persona's situation, the problem shown, the product in use).
3. **Promise**: a reason to keep watching (a question, a claim to be proven, a before state, a number with a source, an unfinished action).

### Hook frame types (pick by awareness level)

| Hook frame | What is on screen at 0.0 s | Best awareness level | Watch outs |
|------------|----------------------------|----------------------|-----------|
| Product in action | Hands using the product, macro, already moving | Solution and product aware | Must be the real product |
| Problem shot | The pain visible (mess, error screen, sore knee, empty calendar) | Unaware, problem aware | No personal attribute phrasing on Meta ("Are you overweight?") |
| Result first | The end state, then rewind | Problem and solution aware | Only real results; typicality disclosure where required |
| Direct address | A person looking at camera mid-sentence | All; strongest on TikTok and Reels | No synthetic person presented as a customer |
| Text-led question | Bold 3 to 6 word question over a relevant visual | Problem aware | Text must sit inside the safe zone |
| Pattern interrupt | Unexpected scale, object, motion or sound tied to the message | Unaware | Must connect to the product by second 3 or it is bait |
| Comparison split | Two halves: old way vs new way | Solution aware | Comparative claims need substantiation; competitor names need legal review |
| Unboxing hands | Package opening, first reveal | Product aware, gifting | Keep it fast; reveal by second 2 |
| POV | First person camera in the situation | Unaware, problem aware | Natural sound helps |
| Green screen over source | Creator over a review, article or Reddit post | Problem and solution aware | Rights and accuracy of the background source |
| ASMR or sound hook | Close sound of the product (pour, click, crunch) | Product aware, food, beauty, tactile | Needs sound on placements |
| Offer first | Price or discount at 0.0 s | Most aware, retargeting | Weak for cold audiences |

### First frame rules (QA blockers in bold)

- **Frame 1 is not black, not a logo, not a fade in.** Motion or a strong still subject from frame 1.
- **Hook text (if any) inside the safe zone and readable on a phone at arm's length.**
- Product or problem visible by 2.0 s.
- The first frame doubles as the thumbnail on many placements. Choose it deliberately; export it as a PNG with the delivery.
- On YouTube in-stream, put the brand or product in the first 5 s (ABCD); on TikTok and Reels, the product or creator carries the brand rather than a logo card.

## 3. Structure by length

| Length | Use | Beat template (seconds) |
|--------|-----|-------------------------|
| 6 s | YouTube bumper, Stories punch, retargeting reminder | 0 to 2 hook plus product, 2 to 5 one benefit or offer, 5 to 6 brand and CTA |
| 9 to 15 s | TikTok and Reels cold traffic tests, Shorts, Meta Feed | 0 to 2 hook, 2 to 8 proof (one demonstration), 8 to 12 benefit or offer, 12 to 15 CTA and end card |
| 15 to 30 s | Default performance length on Meta, TikTok, Shorts, LinkedIn | 0 to 2 hook, 2 to 6 problem, 6 to 18 demo or proof, 18 to 24 objection or social proof, 24 to 30 offer and CTA |
| 30 to 60 s | UGC testimonials (real), founder stories, explainers, listicles, YouTube in-stream | 0 to 3 hook, 3 to 10 setup, 10 to 40 three proof beats, 40 to 50 offer, 50 to 60 CTA |
| 60 s plus | YouTube skippable in-stream, B2B explainers, founder long form, CTV is 15 or 30 | Hook in 0 to 5 (skip button at 5 s), chapters every 15 to 20 s, CTA at 50 percent and at the end |

Length planning rules:
- Let the proof set the length. One demonstration needs 5 to 8 s; three proofs need 25 to 40 s.
- Deliver at least two lengths for every concept that will run on more than one platform (for example 15 s and 30 s).
- TikTok in-feed length guidance conflicts: older TikTok guidance cited 21 to 34 s as a sweet spot; 2026 guides recommend 9 to 15 s or 15 to 30 s [Contested]. Test 15 s against 30 s on the same hook.
- YouTube auto shortens Demand Gen videos with "shorter video" enhancements unless opted out (since 2025-03-10) [Official via PPC News Feed, 2025]. Deliver your own short versions so the machine does not cut the claim off.

## 4. Pacing

| Platform | Shot length in first 5 s | Shot length after 5 s | Notes [Practitioner consensus] |
|----------|--------------------------|----------------------|------|
| TikTok, Reels, Shorts | 0.8 to 2.0 s | 1.5 to 3.5 s | Jump cuts are native; remove breaths and dead air |
| Meta Feed 4:5 | 1.5 to 2.5 s | 2 to 4 s | Many viewers are sound off; text carries more |
| YouTube in-stream | 2 to 3 s | 2.5 to 5 s | Earn the first 5 s, then calmer pacing is fine |
| LinkedIn | 2 to 4 s | 3 to 5 s | Readable charts and UI need longer holds |
| CTV 16:9 | 3 to 5 s | 3 to 6 s | Big screen, lean back, sound on |

Rules:
- Pay off the hook promise by second 5 or 6. A hook that is not paid off is bait and hurts hold rate.
- One pattern break every 5 to 8 s (scale change, cut to silence, new visual medium, a reveal).
- Shot lengths vary. If more than 60 percent of shots have the same duration, the edit feels mechanical (adapted from the saas-motion-kit variety rules).
- Never use a progress bar or a timeline scrubber as decoration; it reads as a screen recording.

## 5. Captions and on-screen text

Two text layers, designed separately:

| Layer | Job | Rules |
|-------|-----|-------|
| Hook and beat text (headlines) | Carry the message with sound off | Max 6 words per beat, 1 to 2 lines, large, inside the safe zone, on screen at least 1.5 s or 0.3 s per word, whichever is longer |
| Spoken word captions | Accessibility and sound off comprehension | Verbatim to the audio, 1 to 2 lines, 18 to 26 characters per line on 9:16, chunked by phrase, synced within 100 ms |

Caption style defaults for 1080 px wide verticals [Practitioner consensus]:
- Font size 56 to 72 px, bold sans with full Latin Extended coverage, white with a 4 to 6 px black outline or a 60 to 80 percent opaque box.
- Position: above the bottom UI band (for Meta 9:16 the lowest caption line sits above y = 1248 px; for TikTok above y = 1440 px). Never over the face or the product.
- Kinetic word-by-word captions (TikTok style) suit UGC and talking heads; static phrase captions suit demos and B2B.
- TikTok suggests 5 to 10 words per second when using text [Official, TikTok Ads Help]. For captions, keep reading speed under about 3 words per second for comprehension [Practitioner consensus].

Platform caption handling:

| Platform | Burn in? | Sidecar? |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Meta Reels and Stories | Yes (Facebook Reels ads do not support auto captioning per 2026 spec guides) | Meta auto captions exist for some Feed video; do not rely on them |
| TikTok | Yes, or TikTok auto captions on organic and Spark posts | Not for ads |
| YouTube | Optional burn in for Shorts | Upload SRT for in-stream and long form |
| LinkedIn | Burn in or upload SRT (English only supported per LinkedIn help) | SRT via Campaign Manager or API |
| CTV | Usually no burn in; follow publisher spec | Closed captions per publisher |

Uppercase in Turkish and Azerbaijani: type the uppercase text yourself (i to İ, ı to I) or use `--upper tr` in [ffmpeg_deliver.py](../scripts/ffmpeg_deliver.py). CSS `text-transform` and naive uppercase functions get this wrong.

## 6. Sound

| Placement | Default sound plan |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Reels, TikTok, Shorts | Sound on: voice plus music, or native creator audio; sound design on product actions; captions still burned in |
| Meta Feed, LinkedIn | Sound off first: the ad must work muted; voice and music still help when tapped |
| YouTube in-stream, CTV | Sound on; voice carries the brand name and CTA |
| Stories | Mixed; captions required |

Rules:
- Duck music 12 to 18 dB under voice; keep voice intelligible on phone speakers (high-pass at 80 to 100 Hz, presence boost around 3 to 5 kHz) [Practitioner consensus].
- Licensed music only. Trending organic sounds are usually not licensed for ads ([Avatars, voice and music](avatars-voice-and-music.md)).
- Loudness targets in [Platform specs and safe zones](platform-specs-and-safe-zones.md) section 8.
- A cut to silence is a strong pattern break before a reveal; use once per ad.

## 7. Branding

| Platform | Brand timing | How |
|----------|-------------|-----|
| TikTok, Reels | Product visible by 2 s; brand name spoken or shown by mid ad | Product, packaging, creator saying the name; logo only on the end card |
| YouTube in-stream | First 5 s (ABCD) | Product in use plus brand mention in VO; small logo bug allowed |
| Shorts | Early and often, test 4 s vs later (Wolt trade-off) | Spoken brand plus product |
| LinkedIn | Early | Logo and company identity, real employees |
| CTV | Within first 5 to 10 s and at the end | Logo, VO |

## 8. End cards and CTAs

- Hold the end card 2 to 3 s (use `--hold-last` in the delivery script when the edit ends early).
- One action verb that matches the platform CTA button ("Shop now", "Get the quote", "Start free trial").
- Offer text exactly as approved by `offer-strategy` or in `PROJECT_BRIEF.md`; include required legal lines (terms, dates, "while supplies last" only if true).
- No fake buttons, fake play icons, fake notifications or system dialogs (policy trap on all platforms).
- Countdown visuals only for real deadlines; record the end date in the manifest so the channel agent pauses the ad.
- Keep end card text out of the bottom UI band on 9:16.
- Produce end card variants as a CTA family (`cta1`, `cta2`, `cta3`): price led, benefit led, deadline led, guarantee led.

## 9. Native grammar by platform

| Platform | Looks native when | Looks like an ad (avoid) |
|----------|-------------------|--------------------------|
| TikTok | Creator to camera, phone footage, native text style, trends or sounds that fit, fast jump cuts, humor | Logo intro, letterboxed 16:9, stock music bed only, polished studio look on cold traffic |
| Instagram Reels | Similar to TikTok, slightly more aesthetic; music or VO; captions | Reposted TikTok with watermark (do not reuse watermarked exports) |
| Facebook Feed 4:5 | Clear headline text, product demo, testimonial, readable without sound | Tiny text, 9:16 file cropped badly into 4:5 |
| Stories | Full screen, tap through pacing, sticker-like text, direct CTA | Important text near the top bar or reply bar |
| YouTube Shorts | Creator style, vertical, hook in 1 s, spoken brand | Horizontal video letterboxed into 9:16 |
| YouTube in-stream | Story or demo, brand early, CTA spoken and on screen, 16:9 | Waiting until second 20 to reveal the product |
| Demand Gen | Supply 16:9, 1:1, 9:16 (and 4:5) versions | One ratio only (Google auto adapts badly) |
| LinkedIn | Role specific problem, data with source, real people, captions, 15 to 30 s | Consumer style memes for senior B2B buyers (test, do not assume) |
| CTV | Broadcast quality, 15 or 30 s, 16:9, loud and clear VO | Vertical or UGC phone footage without treatment |

## 10. One message rule

One ad, one message. If the script needs "and" to state the message, split it into two ads. Variants test different ways of landing the same message (hooks, proof, CTA), not different messages. [saas-motion-kit playbook; Practitioner consensus]

## 11. Script templates (copy and fill)

**15 s problem solution (UGC or VO over demo)**
```
0.0 to 2.0  HOOK      Visual: <problem shot or product in action>  Text: "<3 to 6 words>"  VO: "<hook line>"
2.0 to 5.0  PROBLEM   Visual: <the pain, specific>                 VO: "<customer words from VOC bank>"
5.0 to 11.0 PROOF     Visual: <real demo, one continuous action>   Text: "<claim ID CL-xxx>"  VO: "<what it does>"
11.0 to 15.0 CTA      Visual: <end card, offer>                    Text: "<offer>"  VO: "<action verb + offer>"
```

**30 s UGC demo (real creator, real experience)**
```
0 to 2   Hook to camera with product in hand
2 to 6   "I tried <failed solutions>" (VOC language)
6 to 18  Demo in 2 to 3 shots: open, use, result (real, unedited result)
18 to 24 Objection handled ("I thought it would <objection> but <fact>")
24 to 30 Offer and CTA, creator says the brand and the action; end card 2.5 s
Disclosure: "Paid partnership" or #ad in the first seconds if the creator is paid
```

**6 s bumper**
```
0 to 2  Product doing its one job (motion from frame 1)
2 to 5  One benefit in 3 to 5 words, spoken and on screen
5 to 6  Logo plus CTA
```

**45 s listicle ("3 reasons")**
```
0 to 3   Hook: "3 reasons <persona> switch to <category>"
3 to 15  Reason 1 with proof shot
15 to 27 Reason 2 with proof shot
27 to 39 Reason 3 with proof shot (strongest last)
39 to 45 Offer and CTA
```

**10 s offer video (code-driven)**
```
0 to 1.5  Product hero in motion plus offer number
1.5 to 6  2 benefits as kinetic type, product rotates or is used
6 to 10   Price, discount, deadline (real), CTA, legal line
```

**30 s B2B product demo (LinkedIn, YouTube)**
```
0 to 3   The role's problem in one line plus UI frame of the pain
3 to 8   Product arrives on the real (clean) or truthful imaginary UI
8 to 22  One workflow in 3 steps, cursor carries the eye, numbers are demo scale and labeled
22 to 27 Proof: customer logo or quote only with written permission; otherwise a sourced metric
27 to 30 CTA: "Book a demo" or "Start free", URL on screen
```
