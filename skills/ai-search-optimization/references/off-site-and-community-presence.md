# Off-site and Community Presence

> Knowledge as of 2026-10. For "best", "top", "vs" and "alternatives" prompts, AI engines mostly cite third-party pages: listicles, reviews, forums, videos and reference sites. The brand wins by being present, accurately and genuinely, on the sources each engine already uses for its category. Manipulation is detectable, often against platform rules and the law, and it poisons the very sources the engines rely on.

## 1. Source mapping (always do this before any off-site work)

Procedure:
1. Export cited URLs for the priority prompt set from the tracking tool (or manual runs) for the last 30 days, per engine.
2. Classify each cited domain:

| Class | Examples | Typical lever |
|-------|----------|---------------|
| Editorial listicle or review | Trade media, review blogs, affiliate sites | PR, product sampling, affiliate partnership, data pitch |
| Review platform | G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, Yelp, Google reviews | Review program, profile completeness |
| UGC forum | Reddit, Quora, niche forums, Stack Exchange | Genuine expert participation, community programs |
| Video | YouTube, TikTok (rarely cited) | Owned videos, creator reviews |
| Reference | Wikipedia, Wikidata, government, standards bodies | Entity work, compliance with COI rules |
| Professional network | LinkedIn posts and articles | Executive and employee publishing |
| Competitor owned | Competitor comparison pages | Own comparison content, correct factual errors |
| Own site | Your domain | Content engineering |
| Marketplaces | Amazon, app stores, Etsy | Listing quality and reviews |

3. Build the source opportunity table:

| Domain | Class | Engines citing | Prompts citing (count) | Brand mentioned there? | Accuracy | Reachability (easy, medium, hard) | Action | Owner |
|--------|-------|----------------|------------------------|-----------------------|----------|-----------------------------------|--------|-------|

4. Prioritize: domains cited across many prompts and engines, where the brand is absent or wrong, and reachability is easy or medium.
5. Re-run the mapping quarterly. Source mixes shift (Reddit's ChatGPT share fell sharply in 2025-09; YouTube rose through 2025 to 2026) [Study, 2025 to 2026].

## 3. Reddit

### 3.1 Why it matters
Reddit content is licensed to Google (2024) and OpenAI (2024), ranks well in Google, and is heavily cited by Perplexity and Google AI features [Official for licensing deals; Study for citations].

### 3.2 Ethical participation playbook
1. Read the subreddit rules. Many ban self-promotion or require flair.
2. Use real, named accounts for employees, with affiliation disclosed in the profile and in any comment that mentions the brand ("I work at Acme").
3. Answer questions where your expertise is real, including when the best answer is a competitor or a free method. Mention the brand only when directly relevant.
4. Run official AMAs with moderator approval when there is genuine news or expertise.
5. Monitor brand mentions (Reddit search, social listening). Respond to complaints with help, not spin.
6. Use Reddit's official business tools (Reddit Pro, Reddit Ads) for brand presence and paid reach. Paid placement is labeled and separate.
7. Track which threads get cited for your prompts. Improve the brand's genuine presence in those threads (answer follow-up questions, correct factual errors with disclosure).

### 3.3 What manipulation looks like (never do it)
| Tactic | Why it fails |
|--------|-------------|
| Buying aged accounts or upvotes | Violates Reddit's content policy; accounts and domains get banned; threads get removed |
| Undisclosed employees or agencies posting as customers | Deceptive endorsement under FTC guides; reputational damage when exposed |
| Seeding fake "what's the best X" threads answered by sock puppets | Moderators and users detect patterns; Reddit removes; engines may drop the thread |
| Mass commenting with brand links | Spam filters, shadow removal |
| AI-generated comment farms | Same as above, plus scaled abuse |

If a client or agency proposes any of these, decline, log it in the journal, and propose the ethical alternative.

## 4. YouTube and video

1. YouTube is a growing citation source for AI Overviews, AI Mode and ChatGPT [Study, 2026].
2. Publish videos that answer priority prompts: comparisons, how-tos, reviews, demos, pricing walkthroughs.
3. Titles state the question and entity ("Acme vs Globex payroll: real pricing for 20 employees (2026)").
4. Descriptions: 2 to 4 sentence summary with the key facts, chapters with descriptive titles, links to sources.
5. Upload accurate captions or correct auto-captions. Transcripts are the text engines can use [Practitioner consensus].
6. Earn independent creator reviews (send product, no script control, disclosure per FTC). Independent reviews carry more weight than owned videos.
7. Embed key videos on the matching site page with a text summary.
8. Hand off production to `creative-strategy`.

## 6. Editorial lists and review publishers

1. From the source map, list the 20 to 50 third-party articles cited most for category prompts.
2. For each: who publishes it, last updated date, whether the brand is included and accurate, the inclusion criteria, contact path (editor, author, affiliate program).
3. Outreach with substance: what changed (new features, pricing, data), why readers benefit, offer of a demo or sample, correction of factual errors with evidence.
4. Affiliate relationships are legitimate when disclosed by the publisher. Paying for undisclosed placement is not.
5. Track inclusion status quarterly. Lists update; re-pitch when the brand has news.

Outreach template (correction):
```
Subject: Correction for "<article title>" (<brand> pricing changed 2026-09)

Hi <name>,
Your guide "<title>" lists <brand> at $X per month. Our current public
pricing (since 2026-09-01) is $Y, documented here: <pricing URL>.
Happy to provide a test account if you are updating the guide.
<name>, <role> at <brand>
```

Outreach template (inclusion):
```
Subject: Data for your "<category> tools" guide

Hi <name>,
You updated "<title>" in <month>. Two things your readers may find useful:
1. <specific differentiator with proof>
2. <original data point with link>
If you are testing tools for the next update, I can set up an account and
answer questions. No obligation to include us.
```

## 8. Measurement of off-site work

| Metric | How |
|--------|-----|
| Mentions on priority cited domains | Count of priority domains where the brand appears accurately (target: up quarter over quarter) |
| New third-party mentions per month | Mention monitoring tool or search operators |
| Brand presence in cited threads and lists | From source map refresh |
| Effect on AI visibility | Mention rate for prompts where those sources are cited, before vs after (4 to 12 week lag) |
| Referral traffic from those sources | GA4 referral report |

Attribution caution: mentions and AI visibility both rise with general brand growth. Use prompt clusters where only the off-site action changed as the comparison.

## 9. Red lines (non-negotiable)

1. No fake reviews, testimonials, accounts or endorsements.
2. No undisclosed paid placements or employee posts.
3. No AI comment or post farms.
4. No vote manipulation.
5. No content designed to deceive engines (hidden text, cloaking, prompt injection).
6. Escalate any request to do these to the human, and record it in the journal.
