# Trendyol and Hepsiburada (Turkey)

> Knowledge as of 2026-10. Trendyol and Hepsiburada publish binding commission rates, cargo tariffs and campaign terms inside their seller panels (Trendyol Satıcı Paneli, Hepsiburada Merchant Portal), and the third-party guides that quote them disagree. Treat every rate below as a starting assumption until read from the account. Turkish law changed several times in 2025 and 2026; legal items go to `compliance` before action.

## 2. Commissions and fees

### 2.1 Trendyol
| Item | 2026 state | Label |
|------|-----------|-------|
| Category commission | About 5% (digital gift cards) to 27% (phone spare parts); phones about 7%; shoes about 21.5% to 23.5% | [Secondary, Sentos 2026-07 and Dopigo; Contested ranges] |
| Commission base | Contested: some sources say commission is on the VAT-exclusive price with VAT invoiced on the commission, others say VAT-inclusive | [Contested] Check "Anlaşma Bilgileri" in the panel |
| Dynamic commission | Reported in some subcategories from 2026, with short-term changes during campaigns | [Unverified] |
| Cargo (Trendyol Express) | Charged by desi (volumetric weight); for example 0 to 2 desi TRY 81.95 plus VAT, 5 desi TRY 114.10, 10 desi TRY 164.18 per a 2026-09-08 list | [Secondary, 2026-09; other sources quote lower, older tariffs] |
| Barem (low basket shipping contribution) | Orders under a basket threshold (reported bands 0 to 199.99 and 200 to 349.99 TRY) pay a fixed cargo amount instead of the desi tariff | [Secondary, 2026; Unverified] |
| Service and platform fees | Per order service fee and other deductions per contract | [Unverified] |
| Withholding | 1% e-commerce income tax withholding (stopaj) on seller payouts, in force since 2025-01-01 | [Official, 2024-12; verify current rate] |

Unit economics in TRY must include high inflation: refresh cost and fee inputs monthly, and compare periods in real terms (IAS 29 style restatement or CPI deflation). See [Unit economics](marketplace-unit-economics.md) section 6.

## 3. Buybox logic (both marketplaces)

Neither marketplace publishes weights. Practitioner sources agree on these drivers [Practitioner consensus, 2026]:

| Driver | Trendyol | Hepsiburada |
|--------|----------|-------------|
| Price | Strongest single factor | Strongest single factor |
| Seller or store score | Seller score (rule of thumb 9.0 or higher; some advise 9.2) built from reviews, returns, complaints, cargo performance | Store score (mağaza puanı) |
| Delivery speed | Fast delivery badge, cargo handover time | Cargo handover time (kargoya teslim süresi) |
| Free shipping | Counts | Counts |
| Campaign participation | Campaign depth and participation influence visibility | Campaign participation |
| Stock and sales history | Continuity | Sales volume, stock continuity |
| Cancellations and returns | Lower is better | Cancellation and return rate explicitly listed |
| Brand authorization | Brand owners and authorized sellers protected through brand registration | Authorization documents required for some brands |

Rules:
- Automated pricing must keep a floor price from the contribution model. The Competition Board made Trendyol's commitments binding on 2024-10-03: Trendyol may not require sellers to use its automated pricing mechanism or give incentives that lead to the same result [Official, Rekabet Kurulu, 2024-10]. Use repricing tools as an option, never as a forced price.
- A brand owner losing the buybox to a reseller on its own product: check price, cargo speed and seller score first; use brand registration and authorized seller tools; never instruct resellers on price (resale price maintenance is enforced by the Turkish Competition Authority).

## 4. Advertising

### 4.1 Trendyol ad products
| Product | What | Settings and notes | Label |
|---------|------|--------------------|-------|
| Ürün reklamı (product ads) | Sponsored placements in listing, recommendations and personalized areas | Satıcı Paneli > Reklam Yönetimi > Yeni Reklam Oluştur; minimum daily budget TRY 10 per the official FAQ | [Official FAQ; Secondary] |
| Mağaza reklamı (store ads) | Promotes the whole store in the app area "Sana Özel Markalar"; automatic targeting | Requires an active store page | [Secondary, 2026] |
| Influencer reklamı | Trendyol selects influencers who share products with collaboration links, labeled #işbirliği | Eligibility depends on 30 day sales and view data [Unverified]; results reported to the seller after the campaign | [Secondary] |
| Meta collaboration ads (CPAS) | Facebook and Instagram ads using Trendyol product data and landing on Trendyol | Measure in Trendyol and Meta reports | [Secondary] |

### 4.2 Hepsiburada (HepsiAd)
- HepsiAd is Hepsiburada's retail media business: sponsored product placements for sellers via the merchant portal, display and brand campaigns, and offsite media including exclusive ad areas on HBO Max Türkiye [Secondary, 2025 to 2026].
- New ad models were launched for Efsane Kasım (November event) [Secondary].
- CPC levels and exact sponsored product settings were not found in public sources [Unverified]; read the panel.

### 4.3 Structure for Turkish marketplace ads
```
Per marketplace, per product group (margin band):
  Product ads for hero SKUs with buybox and stock (daily budget, CPC or target)
  Category or keyword targeting where available
  Store ads only when the store has 20 or more strong SKUs and good ratings
  Influencer or CPAS campaigns as tests with a holdout period
Daily review in campaign periods; weekly otherwise
```
Measure ads against total marketplace sales and contribution (TACoS), because attribution inside the panels is short and not comparable across marketplaces.

## 5. Campaigns and events

| Event | Timing | Notes |
|-------|--------|-------|
| 11.11 | 11 November | Large marketplace event |
| Efsane Kasım and Black Friday equivalents | November | Biggest month; Hepsiburada launches HepsiAd event models |
| Ramazan Bayramı and Kurban Bayramı | Move each year with the lunar calendar | Gifts, clothing, home |
| Anneler Günü | Second Sunday of May | Gifts, cosmetics |
| Okula dönüş | August to September | Stationery, bags |
| Yılbaşı | December | Gifts |

Marketplace funded vs seller funded discounts: read campaign terms for who funds the discount and whether commission is reduced. Joining a campaign usually requires a price at or below the campaign price for the whole period.

Price display rules: since 2026-08-01, discount advertising must use the lowest price applied in the prior 10 days in the same sales channel (Commercial Advertising Regulation amendment, Official Gazette 2026-07-01); price tags use a 10 day lookback since 2025-10-11 [Official, via secondary sources]. Raising prices before an event and then "discounting" breaks these rules.

## 6. Turkish platform law that shapes marketplace work

| Rule | What it means for sellers and the agent | Date | Label |
|------|----------------------------------------|------|-------|
| Law 6563 as amended by Law 7416 | Large intermediaries face limits: caps on advertising and discount budgets (formula from net transaction volume: 2% of the first TRY 30B, CPI indexed, plus a lower rate above; quarterly spending limits), restrictions on selling own brand goods, on using seller data to compete and on forcing sellers into their logistics or payment services; e-commerce licenses for large platforms and sellers | In force since 2023, licenses fully operational 2026 | [Official, 2022; Secondary for details] |
| Sponsorship exclusion raised | Share of sponsorship spend excluded from ad budgets for large platforms raised from 25% to 50%; refurbished and used goods ads up to 5% excluded | 2025 | [Secondary, Paksoy 2025-06] |
| Monetary thresholds updated | Thresholds for 2025 net transaction volumes raised by the 48.07% annual change in e-commerce volume | Ministry announcement 2026-03-02 | [Official, 2026-03] |
| Administrative fines | Fines under Article 12 raised 25.49% for 2026 | 2025-12-25 communique | [Official, 2025-12] |
| Seller identity | Sellers' KEP (registered email) requirement removed; platforms must verify seller identity | 2025-03-08 | [Secondary, 2025] |
| Constitutional Court decision E.2024/187, K.2026/42 | Annulled the intermediary non-liability rule (Article 9/1) as it applies to consumer contracts and a related consumer law exception; marketplaces become liable alongside sellers for defective goods claims | Decided 2026-02-12, published 2026-06-02 (Official Gazette 33268), effective 2027-03-02 | [Secondary, multiple law firms; Official Gazette not read directly] |
| Distance Contracts Regulation amendment | Reshaped withdrawal right and return shipping rules; Ministry kept return shipping cost on the seller after review | Published 2025-05-24, in force 2026-01-01 | [Contested: sources disagree on return cost; verify] |
| Commercial Advertising Regulation amendment | 10 day prior price for discount ads (same channel, including loyalty and conditional prices), influencer and consumer review definitions, transparency for targeted ads by intermediaries | Published 2026-07-01, mostly in force 2026-08-01 | [Official Gazette, via secondary] |
| Draft regulation on cargo liability | Loss and damage responsibility in contracts; marketplaces to set compensation rules between seller and carrier; reported limits on competitor brand use in search and free data portability for sellers | Draft around 2026-08 | [Unverified, single source] |

What changes after 2027-03-02: marketplaces will likely tighten seller onboarding, product documentation and defect handling because they share liability [Practitioner expectation; Unverified]. Prepare: product conformity documents, warranty certificates, clear defect and return handling SLAs.

Commercial messages to customers in Turkey need IYS registration and consent; marketplace buyer data cannot be used for your own marketing (KVKK and marketplace contracts). Route to `lifecycle-crm` and `compliance`.

## 7. Listings on Trendyol and Hepsiburada

| Element | Practice |
|---------|---------|
| Title | Brand + product type + key attribute + variant, in Turkish; avoid keyword lists |
| Attributes | Fill every category attribute; filters drive a large share of discovery [Practitioner consensus] |
| Images | Category specs (white background for main image in most categories; lifestyle images allowed after); show size and use |
| Description | Turkish, factual, with care and use instructions; no unverified claims (health, "en iyi") |
| Variants | Correct size and color grouping; size charts for fashion |
| Q&A | Answer customer questions fast; response speed affects store score [Practitioner consensus] |
| Reviews | Platform review requests only; no incentives (Commercial Advertising Regulation defines consumer reviews from 2026-08) |
| Installments (taksit) | Show and support installment options where allowed by category rules (legal caps on installments for some categories) |
