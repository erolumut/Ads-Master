# Environmental Claims

> Knowledge as of 2026-10. Rule pack for environmental, climate, sustainability, durability and circularity claims in the EU, Germany, Netherlands, UK, US and Turkey. The biggest 2026 change: the EU Empowering Consumers for the Green Transition Directive (2024/825) applies from 2026-09-27, and Turkey moved its green claims guide into binding regulation from 2026-08-01. Any new environmental claim for these markets is NEEDS HUMAN OR LEGAL REVIEW until approved in CLAIMS.md.

## 2. EU rules from 2026-09-27

### 2.1 New blacklist items (always unfair, no case by case test)

| Rule ID | UCPD Annex I item | Practical meaning |
|---------|-------------------|-------------------|
| ENV-EU-01 | 2a: Displaying a sustainability label not based on a certification scheme or not established by public authorities | Self made badges ("Eco Choice", "Planet Approved") are banned unless they come from a third party certification scheme or a public authority |
| ENV-EU-02 | 4a: Making a generic environmental claim where the trader cannot demonstrate recognised top environmental performance relevant to the claim (the Directive's defined term) | "Eco-friendly", "green", "environmentally friendly", "nature's friend", "ecological", "climate friendly", "gentle on the environment", "carbon friendly", "biobased" style wording is banned unless backed by, for example, the EU Ecolabel or an equivalent official scheme for that performance |
| ENV-EU-03 | 4b: Making an environmental claim about the entire product or the trader's entire business when it concerns only a certain aspect | "Made with recycled materials" when only the packaging is recycled |
| ENV-EU-04 | 4c: Claiming, based on offsetting greenhouse gas emissions, that a product has a neutral, reduced or positive impact on the environment | "Climate neutral", "carbon neutral", "CO2 compensated", "net zero product" based on credits are banned for products. Claims about the company's own reductions remain possible under the general rules |
| ENV-EU-05 | 10a: Presenting requirements imposed by law on all products in the category as a distinctive feature | "Free from CFCs", "REACH compliant" as a selling point |
| ENV-EU-06 | 23d to 23i: durability and repair practices: withholding information that a software update will harm functionality; presenting a software update as necessary when it only adds features; commercial communication about a product with a feature introduced to limit durability; falsely claiming durability in normal use; presenting goods as repairable when they are not; inducing earlier replacement of consumables; withholding that functionality is impaired with third party consumables | Durability, update and repair claims need evidence |

### 2.2 New misleading action and omission rules (case by case)

| Rule ID | Rule |
|---------|------|
| ENV-EU-07 | Future environmental performance claims ("net zero by 2040", "100% recyclable packaging by 2030") are misleading unless backed by clear, objective, publicly available and verifiable commitments in a detailed and realistic implementation plan with measurable, time bound targets and resources, regularly verified by an independent third party expert whose findings are public (UCPD Art 6(2)(d)) |
| ENV-EU-08 | Advertising benefits for consumers that are irrelevant and do not result from any feature of the product is misleading (UCPD Art 6(2)(e)) |
| ENV-EU-09 | Comparing products on environmental or social characteristics or circularity requires information on the comparison method, the products and suppliers compared, and how the information is kept up to date (UCPD Art 7(7)) |
| ENV-EU-10 | Specific claims remain allowed when accurate, clear, unambiguous and substantiated: name the attribute, the part of the product, the percentage and the standard ("Bottle body made of 80% recycled PET, cap excluded") |

### 2.3 Germany and Netherlands specifics
- Germany: BGH I ZR 98/23 (2024-06-27, Katjes "klimaneutral"): an ambiguous environmental term must be explained in the advertisement itself, not only through a QR code or website. From 2026-09-27, offset based product neutrality claims are banned outright by the UWG blacklist. Competitor and association warnings (Abmahnungen) are the main enforcement path; expect them quickly after the start date. [Official via secondary]
- Netherlands: ACM published sustainability claim guidance and stated the new rules apply from 2026-09-27; the Dutch Advertising Code (SRC) also has an environmental advertising code. [Official]

## 6. Claim decision tree

```
1. Is it an environmental claim at all? (words, images, colours, leaves, globes, labels, brand or product name)
   no -> standard checks
2. Is it generic ("eco", "green", "sustainable", "responsible")?
   EU (from 2026-09-27): BLOCKED unless recognised top performance (EU Ecolabel or official scheme) is documented -> ENV-EU-02
   TR: BLOCKED without explanation and documents -> ENV-TR-01
   UK/US: NEEDS HUMAN OR LEGAL REVIEW; rewrite to specific
3. Is it a product neutrality or reduced impact claim based on offsets?
   EU: BLOCKED (ENV-EU-04). UK/US: qualify (offset scheme, scope) and review. TR: documents and explanation.
4. Is it a sustainability label or badge?
   Not third party certified or public -> BLOCKED in EU (ENV-EU-01)
5. Is it a future target?
   Implementation plan, independent verification, public -> else BLOCKED in EU (ENV-EU-07), TR needs public strategy
6. Is it a specific claim?
   Scope (part vs whole), percentage, standard, evidence fact, date -> APPROVED WITH EDITS if any element missing
7. Is it a comparison?
   Method, products compared, update process disclosed (EU Art 7(7))
```

## 7. Rewrite patterns

| Risky | Compliant direction (only if true and evidenced) |
|-------|------------------------------------------------|
| "Eco-friendly packaging" | "Box made from 100% recycled cardboard (FSC Recycled certified)" |
| "Climate neutral delivery" | "We cut delivery emissions 32% per parcel since 2023 by switching to electric vans in Berlin and Hamburg (verified by X, 2026)" |
| "Sustainable sneakers" | "Upper made from 60% recycled polyester; sole contains no recycled content" |
| "Green energy store" | "Our stores run on electricity from renewable sources under a certified green tariff (supplier, certificate)" |
| "Plastic free" | "Plastic free packaging: no plastic in box, filling or tape" (product itself named if not) |
| "Net zero by 2035" | Only with the public transition plan, interim targets and the independent verifier named |
| Own "Planet Promise" badge | Remove in EU; use EU Ecolabel or a recognised scheme if certified |

## 8. Review checklist

| # | Check | Fail verdict |
|---|-------|--------------|
| 1 | No generic environmental words or imagery without recognised performance (EU) or explanation and documents (TR) | BLOCKED |
| 2 | No product carbon or climate neutrality based on offsets (EU) | BLOCKED |
| 3 | No self made sustainability labels (EU) | BLOCKED |
| 4 | Every specific claim names the part, percentage, standard and has an evidence fact | APPROVED WITH EDITS or BLOCKED |
| 5 | Future targets have a public plan and independent verification | BLOCKED (EU) |
| 6 | No legal requirements presented as distinctive | BLOCKED (EU) |
| 7 | Durability and repairability claims evidenced | NEEDS HUMAN OR LEGAL REVIEW |
| 8 | Brand and product names checked (a name like "EcoPure" is itself a claim in Germany's new definition) | NEEDS HUMAN OR LEGAL REVIEW |
| 9 | California: AB 1305 disclosures published if making neutrality or net zero claims there | NEEDS HUMAN OR LEGAL REVIEW |
| 10 | Feeds, PDPs and old blog posts swept for the same claims (the EU rules apply to existing content still visible after 2026-09-27) | Sweep and fix |

| Copy | Market | Verdict | Rule | Fix |
