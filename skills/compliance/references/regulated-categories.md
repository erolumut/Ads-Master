# Regulated Categories: Finance, Crypto, Credit, Gambling, Alcohol, Dating, Political

> Knowledge as of 2026-10. Every asset in these categories is NEEDS HUMAN OR LEGAL REVIEW unless its exact wording is an Approved row in CLAIMS.md for the market. The agent checks documented rules and platform eligibility; licensing and regulatory approval decisions belong to the human and counsel.

## 6. Dating

1. Platforms: Meta requires prior written permission; Google restricts under dating and companionship rules (no compensated dating, no sexually explicit content); LinkedIn requires authorisation; OpenAI disallows dating.
2. Personal attributes: "Singles in your area", "Meet other divorced women" assert personal attributes (Meta). Rewrite to the service ("Meet people who share your interests").
3. No implied availability of specific people, no fake profiles or stock photos presented as members (UCPD Annex I; FTC deception). Profile counts need a dated fact.
4. 18+ targeting; no imagery that appeals to minors.

Cause marketing rule: keep brand purpose content product linked and factual, avoid calls to political action, and in the EU assume issue classification risk on Meta and Google. Route anything near an election, referendum or bill to the human.

## 8. Review procedure for regulated categories

1. Confirm the advertiser holds the licence or authorisation for every market targeted (record licence numbers as PRODUCT_FACTS rows with expiry).
2. Confirm platform eligibility (matrix above) and that certifications or permissions are active (channel agent checks the account).
3. Check mandatory wording per market (risk warnings, representative examples, "Borrowing money costs money", MiCA statement, responsible gambling messages, alcohol health messages).
4. Check targeting constraints (age, special ad category, geography) with the channel agent.
5. Check the landing page carries the same mandatory information.
6. Verdict: the agent can confirm documented formal requirements; final sign-off is human or counsel (NEEDS HUMAN OR LEGAL REVIEW) for every new asset.
