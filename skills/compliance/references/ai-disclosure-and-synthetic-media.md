# AI Disclosure and Synthetic Media

> Knowledge as of 2026-10. Rule pack for AI generated or altered images, video, voice and text in ads and marketing, synthetic people (avatars, virtual influencers, cloned voices), chatbots that talk to customers, and claims about AI products. Production workflow lives in creative-strategy and video-studio; this module decides disclosure and what is not allowed. Several dates here were verified by the creative-strategy package in the same 2026-10 sweep and are cross referenced.

## 1. Hard lines (BLOCKED in every market)

| Rule ID | Never | Why |
|---------|-------|-----|
| AI-ALL-01 | AI generated customers, testimonials, reviews, experts, doctors or "real results" | FTC 16 CFR 465 (fake reviews including AI generated); UCPD Annex I no.23c; DMCC Schedule 20; TR Art 27/12 (2026) |
| AI-ALL-02 | Digital replicas of a real person (voice, face, name) without written consent for that use, duration and territory | Personality and image rights; TR from 2026-08-01 bans AI replicas of real people shown using or recommending a product; US state likeness laws |
| AI-ALL-03 | Celebrity or influencer lookalikes, soundalikes or simulated endorsements | Deception; TikTok prohibits simulated celebrity endorsement [Unverified wording]; scam ad enforcement (Google 2025 Ads Safety Report: 602 million scam related ads) |
| AI-ALL-04 | AI depictions of product results (skin, weight, hair, performance) | Implied efficacy claim not supported by evidence; CAP and ASA (AI imagery that exaggerates efficacy is misleading) |
| AI-ALL-05 | Fake news screenshots, fake UI, fake social proof generated by AI | UCPD; platform deceptive practices rules |
| AI-ALL-06 | Stripping provenance metadata (C2PA) to avoid labels | Circumvention of platform systems |

## 2. European Union: AI Act Article 50

| Rule ID | Obligation | Applies | Source |
|---------|-----------|---------|--------|
| AI-EU-01 | Deployers that generate or manipulate image, audio or video content constituting a deep fake must disclose that it is artificially generated or manipulated. Evidently artistic, creative, satirical or fictional works: disclosure in a way that does not hamper the work | 2026-08-02 | Reg (EU) 2024/1689 Art 50(4) [Official] |
| AI-EU-02 | Providers of generative AI systems mark outputs in a machine readable, detectable format. Systems placed on the market before 2026-08-02 have until 2026-12-02 (Digital Omnibus on AI, Reg (EU) 2026/1744, in force 2026-07-27) | 2026-08-02 / 2026-12-02 | Art 50(2); Reg 2026/1744 [Official] |
| AI-EU-03 | People interacting with an AI system (chatbots, voice agents in sales or support) must be informed unless obvious | 2026-08-02 | Art 50(1) [Official] |
| AI-EU-04 | Commission Guidelines on Article 50, C(2026) 5054 (2026-07-20, non binding): deep fake defined through four cumulative criteria from Art 3(60); disclosure must be perceivable in practice (not buried in terms or menus); one notice at the start may not be enough where people join a video or podcast midway; no retroactive labelling of content generated before 2026-08-02 (voluntary disclosure encouraged) | Guidance | Paul Weiss 2026-07; Stibbe 2026 [Official via secondary] |
| AI-EU-05 | Code of Practice on transparency of AI generated content (2026-06-10, voluntary; about 190 signatories by 2026-07-31): section 1 for providers (marking, detection), section 2 for deployers (deep fake and public interest text labelling). Non signatories must justify their own measures to market surveillance authorities. Whether a standard EU icon is final is [Contested] | Guidance | Commission digital strategy page; Paul Weiss [Official] |
| AI-EU-06 | Penalties for transparency breaches: up to EUR 15 million or 3% of worldwide annual turnover, whichever is higher (lower for SMEs) | | Art 99 [Official] |

Advertiser reading: a realistic AI presenter, AI scene of a real looking place or event, or AI altered footage of a real person is likely a deep fake in an ad. Disclose visibly in the creative ("AI-generated presenter", "Scene created with AI"), for the whole duration or repeatedly in video, in addition to platform labels. Pure product renders, backgrounds and stylised animation are usually not deep fakes, but platform labels may still apply.

Other EU rules that bite: UCPD (an AI image that exaggerates the product is misleading regardless of the AI Act); France's influencer law requires "Image virtuelle" on AI generated images in influencer commercial content [Official, canonical].

## 3. United States

| Rule ID | Rule | Effective | Source |
|---------|------|-----------|--------|
| AI-US-01 | New York General Business Law 396-b: whoever produces or creates an ad must conspicuously disclose a synthetic performer when it has actual knowledge of its use; fines USD 1,000 first violation, USD 5,000 after | 2026-06-09 | Via creative-strategy sources (Manatt, Honigman) [Official law] |
| AI-US-02 | California SB 1050 (Business and Professions Code 17610, signed 2026-09-16): ads that prominently include a synthetic performer need a clear and conspicuous disclosure near the performer, long enough to read, wording substantially similar to "this performance features a synthetic digital performer"; audio only ads included | 2027-01-01 | Via creative-strategy sources (DLA Piper, Davis+Gilbert) [Official law] |
| AI-US-03 | FTC: fake AI reviews and testimonials banned (16 CFR 465); Endorsement Guides apply to virtual influencers; Section 5 deception covers AI imagery implying results | In force | FTC [Official] |
| AI-US-04 | AI capability claims (AI washing): Workado (2025-04, "98% accurate" AI detector tested at 53% on general content; order requires competent and reliable evidence); Air AI (complaint 2025-08, settlement 2026-03, agentic AI and earnings claims); Growth Cave (2026-01); Cox Media Group "Active Listening" action (2026-05); the FTC set aside the Rytr order (2025-12) citing the AI Action Plan, which narrows theories based on speculative misuse but not false capability claims | 2024 to 2026 | FTC; HKLaw 2026-08; DLA Piper [Official] |
| AI-US-05 | Election deepfakes: many states require disclosure on AI altered political ads; platform election ad rules require it | Varies | State laws [verify per state] |

Working rule from creative-strategy: build every synthetic performer ad to the California standard now (visible text near the performer and a spoken line in audio).

## 7. Decision tree

```
1. Does the asset show a realistic person, voice, place or event created or materially altered by AI?
   no  -> go to 4
   yes -> 2
2. Is it presented as real (customer, expert, employee, real event, real result)?
   yes -> BLOCKED (AI-ALL-01, AI-ALL-04)
   no  -> 3
3. Disclosure set:
   EU: visible in-creative disclosure (AI-EU-01), repeated in long video; platform label
   US: NY synthetic performer disclosure now; California standard from 2027-01-01
   TR: Art 18/8 disclosure; never a real person's replica (AI-TR-02)
   UK: disclose when the audience would otherwise be misled (AI-UK-02)
   Political or social issue: platform alteration disclosure, human review
   -> APPROVED WITH EDITS
4. Is AI used only for backgrounds, b-roll, upscaling, translation, captions, layout?
   yes -> platform labels may apply; no extra disclosure usually needed; product depiction must stay accurate
5. Is there a chatbot or voice agent talking to customers? -> tell users they are talking to AI (AI-EU-03), log consent for recorded calls
6. Does the copy claim AI capabilities? -> substantiate (AI-US-04), FTC style evidence file
```

## 8. Disclosure wording templates

| Situation | On screen | Spoken or caption |
|-----------|-----------|-------------------|
| AI presenter (EU, US) | "AI-generated presenter" (top corner, whole duration) | "This presenter was created with AI." |
| Synthetic performer (California from 2027) | "This performance features a synthetic digital performer" near the performer | Same line spoken in audio only ads |
| AI scene of a real looking place | "Scene created with AI" | n/a |
| Turkish ad with AI character | "Bu reklamda yapay zekâ ile oluşturulmuş karakter kullanılmıştır." | Same in caption |
| Chatbot greeting | "You are chatting with an AI assistant. A human can take over at any time." | n/a |
| Cloned brand voice (consented) | Not required by EU unless deep fake of a real person; disclose if a listener could take it as a real endorser | "Voice generated with AI" |

## 9. Consent and records for synthetic people

1. Written consent from the person for each AI use, with scope (channels, markets, duration, edits allowed) and compensation.
2. For stock avatars: licence covering paid advertising in the target markets and the right to modify.
3. Keep the generation record: tool, prompt, date, operator, source assets, provenance data, the disclosure used.
4. Register the asset in `ads-master/creative-library/registry.csv` with an AI flag (creative-strategy owns the registry).
