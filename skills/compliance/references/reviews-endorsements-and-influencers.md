# Reviews, Endorsements and Influencers

> Knowledge as of 2026-10. Rule pack for consumer reviews, ratings, testimonials, endorsements, creator and influencer content, affiliate links and employee advocacy in the EU, UK, US, Turkey, Netherlands, Germany, France and the Gulf. Creative production rules for creators sit with creative-strategy; this pack decides what is lawful and what disclosure each market needs.

## 1. Reviews and ratings

| Rule ID | Rule | Source |
|---------|------|--------|
| REV-EU-01 | Stating that reviews are submitted by consumers who used or bought the product without taking reasonable and proportionate steps to check is always unfair; submitting, commissioning or misrepresenting fake reviews or social endorsements is always unfair | UCPD Annex I no.23b and 23c (Omnibus 2019/2161) [Official] |
| REV-EU-02 | Where a trader gives access to consumer reviews, information on whether and how it ensures reviews come from real users is material information (publish a "How we verify reviews" note) | UCPD Art 7(6) [Official] |
| REV-UK-01 | DMCC Act Schedule 20 bans fake reviews, commissioning or incentivising fake reviews, and misleading presentation of reviews (including suppressing negatives); CMA investigations opened 2026-03-27 (incentivised reviews, suppressed negatives, staff reviews, inflated ratings); Pasta Evangelists is investigated over discounts for five star reviews without disclosure | DMCC Act; HSF Kramer 2026-03 [Official via secondary] |
| REV-US-01 | FTC Rule on the Use of Consumer Reviews and Testimonials (16 CFR 465, effective 2024-10-21) prohibits: fake or false reviews and testimonials (including AI generated or attributed to people who do not exist or lack the experience); buying reviews conditioned on sentiment; undisclosed insider reviews; company controlled "independent" review sites; suppressing reviews by threats or false accusations; buying or selling fake social media indicators (followers, views) for commercial purposes. Civil penalties per violation (USD 53,088 in the 2025 adjustment; check the current figure) | FTC 2024-08-14 [Official] |
| REV-US-02 | Enforcement: warning letters to 10 companies (2025-12-22: property managers, personal injury firms, an accountant); TruHeight final order 2026-07 (employees and incentivised five star reviews; USD 750,000 paid); DOJ and Illinois v. Premium Home Service (2026-05-11, fake listings and reviews; motion to dismiss pending) | DLA Piper 2026-07; FTC 2026-05 and 2026-07 [Official] |
| REV-TR-01 | From 2026-08-01: unverified consumer reviews may not be used in ads; agreements to produce fake reviews are prohibited; consumer reviews with health claims that breach the rules may not be published | Ticari Reklam Yönetmeliği Art 28/B as amended [Official via law firm summaries] |
| REV-PLAT-01 | Google Merchant Center and Google Business Profile ban review gating and incentives for positive reviews; seller ratings must come from approved sources | Google policies [Official] |

Review collection rules (all markets):
1. Ask every customer, not only happy ones (no gating by satisfaction score).
2. Incentives only if neutral (same reward for any review), disclosed on the review, and allowed by the platform (Amazon, Google and Trustpilot rules are stricter).
3. Never write, edit or delete reviews except for clear published policy reasons (illegal content, personal data), applied equally to positive and negative reviews.
4. Employees, family and agencies disclose their connection or do not review.
5. Show the review source, count and date with any star rating in ads.
6. Never generate reviews or testimonials with AI. Summaries of real reviews must be labelled as summaries and be representative.

## 4. Creator contract and brief clauses (minimum)

1. Disclosure label per market (table above), position, and that the platform paid partnership tool is also switched on.
2. Approved claims only: attach the CLAIMS.md extract; no health, financial or environmental claims beyond it; no comparative claims.
3. Honest opinion and actual use: the creator must have used the product for a stated period before posting.
4. No before and after or results claims without the brand's evidence and compliance approval.
5. No AI cloning of the creator's voice or face without separate written consent; no AI generated "customers".
6. Age: creators and audiences 18+ for alcohol (UK: performers 25+), gambling, weight loss, supplements; no creators who appeal mainly to children for restricted categories.
7. Licence and permit warranties (UAE permit, Saudi Mawthooq, Dutch CvdM registration where applicable).
8. Usage rights for paid amplification (whitelisting, Spark Ads, partnership ads) with duration and territories.
9. Take down within 24 hours on request; brand monitors and corrects posts.
10. Records: keep the brief, approvals, the final post and the disclosure screenshot for the review log.

## 5. Affiliates, employees and brand ambassadors

| Situation | Rule |
|-----------|------|
| Affiliate content and coupon sites | Material connection disclosed near the link; the brand is responsible for affiliates' claims (FTC, CAP, UCPD); ban affiliates from bidding on unapproved claims |
| Employee advocacy on LinkedIn or X | Employees disclose they work for the company when posting about its products; no fake customer personas |
| Customer referral programs | Referral rewards are a material connection; referred reviews must disclose; Turkey SPK bans referral rewards for crypto platforms |
| UGC licensed from customers | Written permission; the post must reflect the customer's real experience; no edits that change meaning |
| AI or virtual influencers | Endorser rules apply (FTC); EU AI Act Art 50 and TR 2026 rules require disclosure where realistic; never present as a real customer |

## 9. Review program audit steps

1. Export review platform settings: invitation rules, filters, moderation policy, incentive settings.
2. Pull 90 days of invitations: were all buyers invited, or only high NPS buyers (gating)?
3. Pull moderation logs: rejected reviews by star rating; negative reviews removed at a higher rate is suppression.
4. Check incentives: identical reward for any review, disclosed on the review, allowed by the platform.
5. Check staff and agency reviews: none, or disclosed.
6. Check syndication: reviews shown on product A that were written for product B.
7. Check ad use: star ratings in ads cite source, count and date.
8. Write findings and remediation; anything systemic is a stop condition for review based ads.

## 10. Common expensive mistakes

- Paying staff or vendors for five star reviews, or giving discounts only for five star reviews (FTC TruHeight 2026; CMA Pasta Evangelists investigation 2026).
- Showing an average rating in ads that excludes the platform where ratings are worse.
- Creator disclosure only in the platform tag, or only at the end of a long caption.
- Reusing a creator's post as a paid ad after the usage rights expired.
- AI generated "UGC" presented as real customer content.
- Not briefing Turkish creators on the 2026 label words ("reklam", "tanıtım") and first screen placement.
