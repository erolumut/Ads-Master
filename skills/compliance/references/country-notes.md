# Country Notes

> Knowledge as of 2026-10. Market by market map of regulators, core advertising laws, enforcement style and the 2025 to 2026 changes that matter for marketing review. Rule IDs point to the topic packs. This is orientation for the reviewer, not legal advice; a market not listed here is NEEDS HUMAN OR LEGAL REVIEW for every claim until counsel briefs the registry.

## 1. European Union (baseline for all member states)

| Area | Instrument | Notes |
|------|-----------|-------|
| Unfair practices | UCPD 2005/29/EC as amended by Omnibus 2019/2161 and ECGT 2024/825 | Blacklist (Annex I), misleading actions and omissions, aggressive practices; fines for widespread infringements at least 4% of turnover or EUR 2 million |
| Price indication | Directive 98/6/EC Art 6a | 30 day prior price (PRICE-EU-01) |
| Consumer rights | CRD 2011/83/EU, withdrawal button from 2026-06-19 | SUB-EU-01 |
| Comparative ads | Directive 2006/114/EC | COMP-EU-01 |
| Food claims and labelling | Reg 1924/2006, Reg 1169/2011, Reg 2018/848, Reg 2026/1739 (meat names from 2029) | Food pack |
| Cosmetics | Reg 1223/2009, Reg 655/2013 | Health pack |
| Medicines and devices | Directive 2001/83/EC, Reg 2017/745 | Health pack |
| Finance | MiFID II, CCD2 2023/2225 (from 2026-11-20), MiCA 2023/1114 | Regulated pack |
| Political ads | Reg 2024/900 TTPA (since 2025-10-10) | Regulated pack |
| AI | Reg 2024/1689 Art 50 (since 2026-08-02), Reg 2026/1744 | AI pack |
| Digital services | DSA 2022/2065 Art 26 (ad transparency, no special category profiling), Art 28 (minors) | Privacy pack |
| Privacy | GDPR, ePrivacy Directive | Privacy pack |
| Accessibility | EAA 2019/882 (since 2025-06-28) | Privacy pack section 6 |
| Coming | Digital Fairness Act proposal expected Q4 2026 (dark patterns, influencers, addictive design, personalisation, subscriptions); Green Claims Directive blocked [Contested] | Watch list |

Enforcement style: national consumer authorities (CPC network coordinated actions and sweeps), competitors and consumer associations in civil courts, self regulatory bodies (EASA members). The CPC network ran a prices sweep in late 2025 (results 2026-03-26) and launched action on in-game virtual currencies against nine game companies (2026-09-30).

## 2. Germany

| Area | Key points |
|------|-----------|
| Regulators and enforcers | No central ad regulator: Wettbewerbszentrale, consumer associations (Verbraucherzentralen) and competitors send warnings (Abmahnungen) and sue for injunctions; courts are fast; Bundeskartellamt has limited consumer powers; BaFin (finance); GGL (gambling); BNetzA (calls) |
| Unfair competition | UWG, amended by the Third UWG Amendment Act (promulgated 2026-02-19, environmental rules from 2026-09-27; definition of environmental claim covers images, symbols, brand and company names; fines up to 4% of turnover for larger undertakings) |
| Prices | PAngV § 11: 30 day lowest price for reductions; RRP strike-through prices split between OLG Düsseldorf (2025-12-18, unlawful when it looks like the retailer's own reduction) and OLG Köln (2026-06-15, lawful); BGH pending |
| Health | HWG restricts health advertising (medicines, devices, treatments, cosmetic surgery imagery); HCVO practice follows Reg 1924/2006 strictly; courts enforce Art 10(3) |
| Food | LFGB; Leitsätze (vegan and vegetarian revised 2024-09) |
| Climate claims | BGH I ZR 98/23 (2024-06-27): "klimaneutral" must be explained in the ad; offset based product neutrality banned from 2026-09-27 |
| Influencers | "Werbung" or "Anzeige" at the start; OLG Karlsruhe 2026-03-03 (provided car is consideration); DDG § 6, MStV |
| Consent | UWG § 7 (email consent; existing customer exception), § 7a (phone consent documentation, 5 years) |
| Subscriptions | BGB § 312k cancellation button; month to month after initial term; § 356a BGB withdrawal button (2026) |
| Accessibility | BFSG (EAA) with market surveillance and private warnings |
| Language | German copy; English "ad" label risky for German audiences |

## 6. Turkey

| Area | Key points |
|------|-----------|
| Regulators | Reklam Kurulu (Advertising Board, Ministry of Trade) under Law 6502: stop orders, correction, administrative fines by medium, interim suspension up to 3 months; the advertiser, agency and influencer can be jointly liable. Reklam Özdenetim Kurulu (ROK, self regulation); RTÜK (broadcast); TİTCK (health claims, cosmetics, medicines); Tarım ve Orman Bakanlığı (food, nutrition claims, supplements registration); SPK (capital markets, crypto); BDDK (banking); KVKK (data protection); İYS (consent registry); Rekabet Kurumu (competition) |
| Core regulation | Ticari Reklam ve Haksız Ticari Uygulamalar Yönetmeliği, comprehensively amended (RG 33297, 2026-07-01, in force 2026-08-01): 10 day reference price for discounts per channel, conditional campaigns covered (Art 14); influencer label "reklam" or "tanıtım" on the first screen with advertiser name (Art 23/A); AI disclosure where it significantly affects economic behaviour or uses human-like digital characters (Art 18/8); ban on AI replicas of real people endorsing (Art 27/12); targeted ad transparency and child profiling ban (Art 25/A); unverified reviews not usable and fake review agreements banned (Art 28/B); environmental claim documentation and no generic terms without explanation (Art 17); supplement rules revised (Art 27); academic titles not to be used misleadingly; illegal games of chance ad ban extended |
| Fines | Fine bands are set per medium and updated yearly; 2026 figures for internet ads conflict between sources (for example TRY 1.08 million to 10.8 million in one summary) [Contested]: check the current tebliğ |
| Health and food | Nutrition claims (TGK Beslenme Beyanları Yönetmeliği 2023); health claims only from TİTCK annexes; supplement ads: no doctors, pharmacists, testimonials, "mucizevi" |
| Green claims | Çevreye İlişkin Beyanlar Kılavuzu (2022) principles now in regulation |
| Prohibited or restricted | Alcohol advertising banned (Law 4250); gambling only state licensed; crypto ads only for SPK licensed providers (Law 7518); prescription medicines; health services promotion tightly limited |
| Messaging | İYS consent for commercial messages; KVKK explicit consent separate; KVKK 2025/1072 bans bundled SMS code consent |
| Practical | Turkish copy for Turkish consumers; keep Turkish diacritics in claims (the checker folds dotted and dotless i); Reklam Kurulu publishes monthly decision summaries: read them for current enforcement themes |

## 8. New market intake (for any country not above)

1. Name the consumer protection authority, the ad self regulator, and the health, finance and data authorities.
2. Ask counsel for: price reduction rule, influencer label wording, comparative ad conditions, health claim regime, environmental claim rules, consent rules for email and SMS, restricted categories, language requirements.
3. Record answers as Blocked and Needs review seed rows with market code and source.
4. Until then: every claim in that market is NEEDS HUMAN OR LEGAL REVIEW.
