# Brand vs Performance

Set a deliberate split between demand creation (brand building) and demand capture (activation), measure both, and change it with evidence.

## 1. Definitions used in Ads Master
| Bucket | Includes | Excludes | Judged on |
|--------|----------|----------|-----------|
| Brand building (demand creation, long term) | Broad reach video and CTV, YouTube reach and awareness, Meta and TikTok reach or awareness objectives, sponsorships, out of home, podcasts, creator reach deals, B2B thought leadership | Retargeting, brand search, conversion objective campaigns | Reach in the category buyer pool, mental availability, share of search, brand lift, base sales in MMM, long term nCAC trend |
| Performance (activation, short term) | Search, Shopping, conversion objective social, retargeting, affiliates, marketplace ads | Reach campaigns | CPA, ROAS, POAS, nCAC, incremental conversions |
| Gray zone | Conversion objective social with broad targeting and brand rich creative | | Treat as performance in the budget; credit some brand effect only with MMM or lift evidence |

## 2. Evidence base
| Source | Finding | Label and caveat |
|--------|---------|------------------|
| Binet and Field, "The Long and the Short of It" (IPA, 2013) | Brand building drives long term growth and efficiency; activation drives short term sales; on average the most effective B2C campaigns in the IPA Databank balanced roughly 60% brand and 40% activation | [Study, 2013] Average across award entered cases; not a prescription for every business |
| Binet and Field, "Media in Focus" (IPA, 2017) | Rechecked the balance in the digital era; short term metrics over-credit activation; the long term balance still matters | [Study, 2017] Same databank caveat |
| Binet and Field, "Effectiveness in Context" (IPA, 2018) | The best balance varies by category, market position and growth stage; new products and smaller brands lean more to activation, market leaders and high consideration categories lean more to brand | [Study, 2018] Check the category tables before quoting a number |
| Binet and Field with LinkedIn B2B Institute, "The 5 Principles of Growth in B2B Marketing" (2019) | B2B benefits from brand too; the suggested average split was close to 46% brand and 54% activation | [Study, 2019] B2B averages; enterprise and long cycles lean higher |
| Dawes, Ehrenberg-Bass for LinkedIn B2B Institute, "The 95:5 Rule" (2021) | In many B2B categories only about 5% of buyers are in market in a given quarter; 95% are future buyers who must be reached before they enter the market | [Study, 2021] Share in market varies with purchase cycle; compute your own (section 4) |
| Sharp, "How Brands Grow" (2010) and Ehrenberg-Bass Institute work | Growth comes mainly from increasing penetration among light and non buyers; mental and physical availability drive it | [Study, 2010] Category dependent; strong for FMCG and mass categories |
| Binet, "Share of search" (IPA EffWorks, 2020) | A brand's share of category branded search tends to lead its market share, making it a cheap brand health tracker | [Study, 2020] Works best in categories with meaningful branded search |
| Field, "The Crisis in Creative Effectiveness" (IPA, 2019) | Short termism in budgets and metrics coincided with falling effectiveness of creative | [Study, 2019] Correlational |
| WARC, "The Multiplier Effect" (2025) | Brand and performance multiply rather than add: moving from performance-only to a mixed approach improved total revenue ROI by 25% to 100% (average about 90%) in the cases studied; secondary write-ups cite about 40% lower ROI when moving from a mix to performance-only (Analytic Partners data); winners differed by how well brand and demand activity were connected, not by spend ratio | [Study, 2025] US-focused; ROI ranges come from modeled case data; secondary write-ups differ on the downside figure (40% vs 20% to 50%) |
| 2025 to 2026 updates | No new IPA or LinkedIn B2B Institute split study was found in a 2026-10-08 search; 2026 articles still cite 60:40 (B2C) and 46:54 (B2B, 2019; one table says 50:50) and some cite a 62:38 update or a 70:30 to 40:60 range without a traceable source | [Unverified] for the 62:38 and range claims; re-check IPA, WARC, LinkedIn B2B Institute, Ehrenberg-Bass, System1, Analytic Partners ROI Genome quarterly |

Contested points:
- [Contested] "60/40 is the rule." For: decades of IPA data. Against: average of large, award entered brands; small and new brands with limited cash cannot wait 6 to 24 months for brand effects; Binet and Field themselves recommend context specific ratios.
- [Contested] "Performance creative builds brand too." For: broad targeting on Meta and TikTok reaches many non buyers and good creative carries brand cues. Against: conversion objectives concentrate delivery on likely converters, so reach in the wider category buyer pool is limited. Settle it with reach data (unique reach vs category buyer pool) and lift or MMM.
- [Contested] "Brand cannot be measured." It can be measured imperfectly: share of search, brand lift studies, geo lift on brand spend, MMM base and halo effects.

## 3. How to set the split
### 3.1 Starting point by tier and stage
| Tier or stage | Brand building share of paid media | Notes |
|---------------|------------------------------------|-------|
| Pre product market fit | 0 to 5% | Learn what message converts first |
| Starter (under $3k) | 0 to 10% | Brand search protection is performance; put brand cues into performance creative |
| Growth ($3k to $30k) | 10 to 25% | Start with YouTube or Meta reach in core geos; measure share of search |
| Scale ($30k to $300k) | 25 to 45% | Add CTV or video at scale; run a geo lift on brand spend |
| Enterprise (over $300k) | 40 to 60% B2C, 40 to 50% B2B | Manage with MMM and long term metrics |

### 3.2 Adjustments
| Factor | Adjust brand share |
|--------|--------------------|
| Long purchase cycle or high consideration (B2B enterprise, auto, finance, travel, education) | +5 to +15 points |
| Market leader defending share | +5 to +10 |
| New product or new category, cash constrained | minus 5 to minus 15 |
| Category branded search flat or declining while ours declines | +5 to +10 |
| Performance channels at their marginal ceiling (budget-allocation.md) | +5 to +10 (creation lifts the ceiling) |
| Heavy dependence on marketplaces where shoppers search by category | +5 (brand search on marketplaces matters) |
| Strong organic or community demand already | minus 5 |

### 3.3 Budget shape
- Fund brand from new money or from performance lines at their marginal ceiling, not from core lines still below their ceiling.
- Commit for at least two quarters before judging. Brand effects build over months.
- Keep the performance base stable while brand ramps, so the effect of brand on search demand and nCAC can be read.

## 4. Compute your own "in market" share
```
Share in market per quarter ~ 1 / (average purchase cycle in quarters)
Example: B2B software replaced every 5 years (20 quarters) -> about 5% in market per quarter.
Example: Coffee bought weekly -> nearly everyone in market every quarter; brand is about salience at the shelf.
```
The lower the in market share, the larger the share of budget that must reach future buyers for future demand.

## 5. Measuring brand investment
| Metric | Tool | Cadence | Owner |
|--------|------|---------|-------|
| Share of search (brand vs competitors) | Google Trends, Keyword Planner, GSC branded impressions | Monthly | market-intel |
| Branded search volume and branded clicks | GSC, Google Ads brand campaign impressions | Weekly | seo, google-ads |
| Direct and branded organic traffic | GA4 | Weekly | measurement |
| Brand lift (ad recall, awareness, consideration) | Meta brand lift, Google brand lift, TikTok brand lift (platform eligibility and minimums apply) | Per campaign | channel agents |
| Geo lift on brand spend (sales in exposed vs control regions) | GeoLift, Meridian, matched markets | Twice a year | measurement |
| MMM base sales and halo | Meridian, Robyn, vendor | Quarterly at Scale tier | measurement |
| AI share of voice (assistants recommending us) | AI visibility tools | Monthly | ai-search-optimization |
| Long term nCAC and price premium | Backend, market-intel price monitoring | Quarterly | growth-orchestrator |

Success criteria for a brand program (write in EXPERIMENTS.md):
- Share of search up X points over 2 quarters, or branded search up Y% vs control geos.
- nCAC in exposed geos not worse than control by more than Z% during ramp, and better after 2 quarters.
- Brand lift significant on the primary question (platform study).

## 7. Worked example: setting a split
Business: DTC home goods, Scale tier, $120k per month paid media, market number three in its category, 8 week average consideration, performance channels near their marginal ceiling.
| Step | Calculation | Result |
|------|-------------|--------|
| Starting point (Scale) | 25 to 45% | midpoint 35% |
| High consideration (8 weeks) | +5 | 40% |
| Performance at marginal ceiling | +5 | 45% |
| Not market leader, cash comfortable | 0 | 45% |
| Cap for first two quarters (ramp) | step from current 10% to 25% in Q1, 35 to 45% in Q2 if KPIs move | 25% now |
Plan: $30k per month brand building (YouTube video reach and CTV in half the regions as a geo test, Meta reach with brand rich creative), performance held at $90k. Read share of search and nCAC in test vs control regions after 12 and 24 weeks.

## 8. Signals of under investment in brand
| Signal | How to see it |
|--------|---------------|
| Branded search volume flat or falling while category search grows | Google Trends, GSC, Keyword Planner |
| Share of search falling vs main competitors | market-intel monthly movement |
| nCAC rising on all channels at the same time, with no tracking or site cause | Monthly review |
| Performance channels saturate at lower budgets than a year ago | Response curves, step tests |
| Price promotions needed to hit volume; price premium shrinking | Backend discount share, market-intel price monitoring |
| AI assistants rarely mention the brand in category prompts | ai-search-optimization baseline |

## 9. Making performance creative carry brand
Even at Starter tier, performance ads can build memory structures at no extra media cost:
- Show distinctive brand assets (logo, color, character, sound, product shape) in the first seconds and throughout, not only in the end card.
- Use a consistent tagline or format across concepts so exposures add up.
- Name the category clearly so the brand links to the buying situation (category entry points).
- Keep brand cues in UGC and creator content (product in hand, brand name spoken).
Hand these rules to creative-strategy; they are cheap and compound.

## 10. Brand search: protection, not brand building
- Bid on brand terms when competitors or resellers bid on them, or when the SERP shows ads, shopping units or AI Overviews above the organic brand result.
- Test incrementality with a brand holdout (pause in a set of regions or hours for 2 to 4 weeks) when no competitor bids; measure total brand clicks (paid plus organic) and conversions [Practitioner consensus].
- Report brand search separately from non brand: it inflates blended ROAS and hides the real cost of new demand.
